# Front of House

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.frontofhouse`

## Full description
Front of House gets hospitality workers hired and paid properly: trial-shift prep for any role, guest-scenario interviews, seasonal and cruise-ship applications, venue knowledge crams, and a decoder for tips, tronc and service charge.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Ace the trial shift — What managers watch in the first hour
• Guest scenario interview — Overbooked, complaint, allergen, drunk guest
• Seasonal & cruise jobs — Ski, resort, cruise, festival: timing and papers
• Venue knowledge cram — Menu, wine list and brand facts before the interview
• Decode the pay — Tips, tronc, service charge, split shifts
