# Gravity Gym

**Category:** Health & Fitness  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.gravitygym`  
**Short description (≤80):** Pull-ups, handstands and levers, one rung at a time

## Full description
Gravity Gym is a calisthenics coach that maps the road from zero pull-ups to muscle-ups, handstands, front levers and pistol squats, with skill sessions, photo form checks and plateau fixes.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Skill roadmap — Every rung from where you are to the skill
• Today's skill session — Skill work while fresh, then strength
• Check my form — Photo of a hold or rep
• Break a plateau — Stalled reps or a skill that will not come
• Test day — Re-measure the profile numbers

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
