# Pulp & Press

**Category:** Food & Drink  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.juicepress`  
**Short description (≤80):** Juicing recipes, produce yields and zero-waste pulp

## Full description
Pulp & Press writes juice recipes for your juicer, calculates how much produce you need, plans a juicing week with storage rules, and turns pulp into crackers, broths and muffins.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Juice recipe — From what's in the fridge
• Produce calculator — How much to buy for X ml
• Juicing week — Batch, store, drink on time
• Use the pulp — Crackers, broth, muffins, dog treats
• Clean & maintain — Your juicer, done right

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
