# Roast Day

**Category:** Food & Drink  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.roastday`

## Full description
Roast Day builds a minute-by-minute countdown for the whole roast — joint, potatoes, veg, Yorkshires and gravy — fits it all in one oven, and turns the leftovers into three more dinners.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• The countdown — From joint weight to serving time
• Fit it all in — Oven shelf plan when everything wants 200°
• Gravy & sauces — From the tray, never from a packet
• Sides that shine — Potatoes, Yorkshires, veg — done right
• Leftover roast — Three more dinners from what's left
