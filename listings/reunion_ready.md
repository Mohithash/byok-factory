# Reunion Ready

**Category:** Events  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.reunionready`  
**Short description (≤80):** Get the old crowd back in one room

## Full description
Reunion Ready plans school, university, family, workplace and service reunions: tracking down the lost, invitations people actually answer, a day that flows, ticket pricing that breaks even and a script for the awkward first ten minutes.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Find the lost — Track people down respectfully
• Invitation & RSVP push — The invite and the follow-ups
• Run-sheet for the day — Timings, kit and memory-lane moments
• Budget & ticket price — Break even at any turnout
• Break the ice — The first 30 minutes, handled

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
