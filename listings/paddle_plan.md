# Paddle Plan

**Category:** Sports  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.paddleplan`  
**Short description (≤80):** Plan the paddle, read the water, sharpen the strokes

## Full description
Paddle Plan builds kayak and canoe trips around tide, wind and your group, packs the safety kit that fits the water, reads a rapid or crossing from a photo, coaches strokes and rolls, and drills the rescues you hope never to need.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Plan a paddle — Tide, wind, timings, escape routes
• Safety kit — What to carry for this water
• Read this water — Photo of a rapid, tide race or crossing
• Fix a stroke or roll — Technique coaching
• Rescue drills — Practise before you need it

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
