# Hand Off

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.handoff`  
**Short description (≤80):** Delegate the work — keep the outcome

## Full description
Hand Off helps overloaded leads write delegation briefs that come back right the first time, set the right level of autonomy, hand over whole areas for good and recover gracefully when delegated work goes sideways.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Write a delegation brief — Outcome, constraints, authority, check-ins
• Set the autonomy level — How much rope for this person and task
• Hand over a whole area — Transfer ownership for good
• It came back wrong — Recover without taking it back
• Weekly delegation check — Am I still hoarding?

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
