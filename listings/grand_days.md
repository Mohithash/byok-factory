# Grand Days

**Category:** Parenting  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.granddays`  
**Short description (≤80):** Be the grandparent they run to

## Full description
Grand Days plans visits and video calls with grandchildren by age, decodes the games and slang they're into, prepares your home for babysitting, and gives you the words for tricky moments with their parents.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Plan a visit — Hours of fun, minus the chaos
• Video-call games — For grandkids far away
• Decode kid culture — Games, apps, slang, trends
• Talk to their parents — Boundaries, rules, hurt feelings
• Babysitting-ready home — Older home, small visitors

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
