# Dungeon Desk

**Category:** Games  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.dungeondesk`  
**Short description (≤80):** Session prep, NPCs and encounters in minutes

## Full description
Dungeon Desk is the game master's prep desk: it preps tonight's session, balances encounters for your party, invents NPCs with voices and secrets, and improvises when players wander off the map.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Prep tonight's session — Outline, scenes, contingencies
• NPC on the spot — Name, voice, secret, hook
• Build an encounter — Balanced for the party
• They went off the rails — Improvise the next hour
• Loot & treasure — Rewards that fit the level

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
