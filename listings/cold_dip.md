# Cold Dip

**Category:** Sports  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.colddip`  
**Short description (≤80):** Cold-water swimming, safely, all year round

## Full description
Cold Dip guides cold-water and winter swimming: acclimatisation plans, safe session lengths for the water temperature, afterdrop and rewarming protocols, and safety checks for your swim spot.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Plan today's dip — Water temp → time in, exit plan
• Acclimatisation plan — From summer to winter without shocks
• Rewarming & afterdrop — The 30 minutes after
• Check a swim spot — Photo → hazards and entry
• Group swim briefing — Lead a safe dip

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
