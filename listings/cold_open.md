# Cold Open

**Category:** Music & Audio  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.coldopen`  
**Short description (≤80):** From podcast idea to published episode, every week

## Full description
Cold Open takes a podcast from idea to published episode: episode outlines with cold opens, interview questions that dig deeper, show notes and titles from a transcript, better sound on your setup, and a launch or growth plan.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Outline an episode — Cold open, segments, beats
• Interview questions — Easy → deep → reflective, with probes
• Show notes & titles — Transcript → titles, notes, chapters
• Launch / growth plan — Weeks, actions, signals
• Sound better — Fix the room, the mic and the guest

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
