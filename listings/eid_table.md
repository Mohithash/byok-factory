# Eid Table

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.eidtable`  
**Short description (≤80):** Eid hosting from Chaand Raat to the last guest

## Full description
Eid Table plans Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha at home: the Eid-day run sheet, a feast menu in your family's cuisine, Eidi and gifts for the kids, open-house waves for a long day of visitors, and qurbani share distribution.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Eid day run sheet — From pre-dawn to the last visit
• Eid feast menu — Dishes, cook timeline, shopping
• Eidi & gifts — Fair amounts by age, nice presentation
• Open house waves — Forty guests, fourteen seats, one day
• Qurbani share plan — Arrange, divide, distribute

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
