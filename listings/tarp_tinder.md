## Full description
Tarp & Tinder teaches wild camping and bushcraft: finding a legal, low-impact pitch, tarp and hammock rigs for the weather, fire lays that light in the damp, water sourcing and treatment, and what to do when the map stops making sense.

WHAT'S INSIDE

• Rig for the weather — Tarp / hammock configurations
• Light a fire that stays lit — Tinder, lay and safety for the conditions
• Water plan — Source, treat, carry
• Map stopped making sense — Calm, stepwise relocation
