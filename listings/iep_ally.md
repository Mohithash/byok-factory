# IEP Ally

**Category:** Parenting  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.iepally`  
**Short description (≤80):** Get your child the school support they're entitled to

## Full description
IEP Ally walks parents through requesting an evaluation, decoding the psych-ed report, choosing accommodations that fit, and walking into the IEP, 504 or EHCP meeting prepared and calm.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Request an evaluation — The letter that starts the clock
• Decode the report — Paste the evaluation → plain English
• Accommodations that fit — From struggles to specifics
• Prep the meeting — Agenda, questions, your script
• Paper trail — Log an incident or call

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
