# Helm Notes

**Category:** Auto & Vehicles  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.boathelm`  
**Short description (≤80):** Skipper your small boat with a plan, not a prayer

## Full description
Helm Notes writes passage plans around tides and weather windows, runs pre-launch checks, troubleshoots outboards and inboards, schedules antifouling and winterising, and gives you the exact words for VHF calls.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Passage plan — Route, tides, weather window
• Pre-launch check — Trailer to water in order
• Engine trouble — Won't start, overheats, stalls
• Season schedule — Antifoul, service, winterise
• Radio & signals — Say it right on VHF

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
