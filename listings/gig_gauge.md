# Gig Gauge

**Category:** Finance  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.giggauge`  
**Short description (≤80):** Know what a shift really pays after fuel, wear and tax

## Full description
Gig Gauge works out what delivery, rideshare and task-app work really pays per hour after fuel, vehicle wear, fees and tax, then plans the hours and zones to hit a weekly target, logs expenses from receipt photos and checks your tax set-aside.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• True hourly rate — A week's earnings, all costs out
• Hit my weekly target — Hours and zones to plan
• Log an expense — Photo of a receipt → the log line
• Tax set-aside check — Am I saving enough for the bill?
• Slow week plan — Demand dropped, bills didn't
