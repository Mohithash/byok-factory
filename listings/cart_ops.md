# Cart Ops

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.cartops`  
**Short description (≤80):** Run your online store like a pro ops team

## Full description
Cart Ops works the back end of an online store: SKU unit economics, shipping and returns policies, abandoned-cart sequences, product-page critiques and stock-out playbooks.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• SKU unit economics — What each product earns after everything
• Shipping & returns policy — Thresholds, who pays, the page copy
• Abandoned-cart sequence — Three emails that bring them back
• Critique a product page — Screenshot → conversion fixes
• Stock-out playbook — Keep selling when the shelf is empty

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
