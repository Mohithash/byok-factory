# Sutta Path

## Full description
Sutta Path opens the Pali Canon, Mahayana sutras and Zen koans with clear summaries, key terms and the meditation practices each text points to — without telling you what to believe.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Study a text — Summary, terms, what to practise
• Practice from a text — Sit with what you just read
• A teaching for this — Bring what's happening in your life
• Decode a term — Dukkha, sunyata, jhana, satori…
• 8-week study plan — A text a week, a practice a week
