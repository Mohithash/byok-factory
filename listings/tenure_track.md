# Tenure Track

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.tenuretrack`  
**Short description (≤80):** Faculty applications from statements to startup package

## Full description
Tenure Track guides PhDs and postdocs through the academic job market: research and teaching statements, the job talk, the two-day campus visit and negotiating the startup package, tuned to R1, teaching-focused and community-college searches.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Research statement — Vision, agenda, fundability
• Teaching statement — Evidence-based, not adjectives
• Plan the job talk — 45 minutes, then the hard questions
• Campus visit — Two days of interviews, meals and one-on-ones
• Negotiate the offer — Startup, salary, teaching load, spousal

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
