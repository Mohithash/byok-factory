# Spec Showdown

**Category:** Shopping  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.specshowdown`  
**Short description (≤80):** Gadget specs decoded into a decision

## Full description
Spec Showdown turns phone, laptop, TV, camera and headphone spec sheets into a plain-English head-to-head, tells you which numbers matter for how you'll use it, and flags the ones that are just marketing.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Head-to-head — Paste 2–3 spec sheets or names
• Decode the jargon — What does this spec actually mean?
• What matters for me — Specs to prioritise for your use
• Upgrade or hold? — Is the new model a real step up from yours?
• Scan a shelf tag — Photo of a box or spec label → verdict

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
