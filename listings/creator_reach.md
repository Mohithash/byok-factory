# Creator Reach

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.creatorreach`  
**Short description (≤80):** Influencer outreach that gets replies and results

## Full description
Creator Reach helps you shortlist the right creators, write outreach that gets replies, brief campaigns clearly, structure fair deals and vet a profile from a screenshot.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Sourcing plan — Who to look for and how to find them
• Outreach message — DM or email that gets a reply
• Campaign brief — One page the creator can follow
• Deal terms — Rates, rights and a simple term sheet
• Vet a profile — Screenshot → fit and red flags

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
