# Buzzer Brain

**Category:** Games  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.buzzerbrain`  
**Short description (≤80):** Train to win pub quizzes and game-show auditions

## Full description
Buzzer Brain trains trivia players rather than hosts: category drills with answers, study sheets for the topics quizzes love, gap analysis from your results, wager and team strategy, and post-quiz reviews.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Drill a category — 20 questions with answers
• Study sheet — The classics, condensed
• Find my gaps — Paste your results
• Wager & buzzer strategy — Play the game, not just the questions
• Learn what we missed — Answers, hooks and siblings

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
