# Fixable

**Category:** House & Home  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.fixable`  
**Short description (≤80):** Fix the appliance instead of binning it

## Full description
Fixable helps you rescue small appliances and gadgets: diagnose from the symptom, decide if a repair is worth it, read the rating plate for parts and manuals, find spares and repair cafés, and check repairability before you buy the next one.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Diagnose it — Symptom → causes, in order
• Worth fixing? — Repair cost vs replacement vs lifetime
• Read the rating plate — Photo → model, parts, search terms
• Find parts & help — Spares, repair cafés, manufacturer routes
• Buy repairable — Check before you buy the next one

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
