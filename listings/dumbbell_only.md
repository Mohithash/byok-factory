# Dumbbell Only

**Category:** Health & Fitness  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.dumbbellonly`  
**Short description (≤80):** Build muscle with one pair of dumbbells and a bench

## Full description
Dumbbell Only writes hypertrophy programmes around the exact dumbbells you own, turns any gym exercise into a dumbbell version, tracks progressive overload from your log and plans deloads before you burn out.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Write my programme — Split, sets, reps, progression
• Swap an exercise — Machine or barbell move into a dumbbell version
• Am I progressing? — Paste your log for one lift
• Specialisation block — Bring up a lagging muscle
• Plan a deload — Recover without losing gains

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
