# Windfall Plan

**Category:** Finance  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.windfallplan`  
**Short description (≤80):** A bonus, inheritance or payout, handled without regret

## Full description
Windfall Plan gives sudden money a plan: a priority waterfall, a 90-day do-nothing-rash checklist, lump-sum versus phased investing, questions for an adviser, and scripts for family who ask.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Where should it go? — Debts, buffer, goals, fun
• The 90-day freeze — Do nothing rash, on purpose
• Lump sum or phased? — All in, or in stages
• Find & question an adviser — Who to hire, what to ask
• When family asks — Scripts for money requests

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
