# Cite Sure

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.citesure`  
**Short description (≤80):** Every reference right, every style, no integrity scare

## Full description
Cite Sure builds and fixes references in APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard, IEEE or Vancouver, checks a bibliography from a photo, converts whole lists between styles and tells you whether a paraphrase is too close to the source.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Build a reference — DOI, URL or scraps → formatted entry
• Check my bibliography — Photo of a reference list → fixes
• Too close to the source? — Paraphrase vs patchwriting check
• Convert a list — Change every entry to another style
• Ask a citation rule — Odd sources, secondary cites, et al.

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
