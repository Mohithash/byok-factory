# Deal Verdict

**Category:** Shopping  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.dealverdict`  
**Short description (≤80):** Know if a sale is real before you tap buy

## Full description
Deal Verdict checks whether a discount is genuine, tells you the best month to buy each thing, stacks cashback, coupons and card perks into one plan, and turns big sale days into a shortlist you won't regret.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Is this deal real? — Paste a deal or snap the price tag
• Best time to buy — The cheapest month for this category
• Stack the savings — Cashback, coupons, cards — in the right order
• Sale-day battle plan — Black Friday or Prime Day without the regret
• Price dropped after I bought — Claim the difference back

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
