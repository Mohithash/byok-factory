# Feather Friend

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.featherfriend`  
**Short description (≤80):** Tame, feed and enrich your parrot or budgie

## Full description
Feather Friend converts seed junkies to healthy chop and pellets, builds step-up and taming plans, decodes screaming, biting and plucking, and audits your home for the fumes and hazards birds can't survive.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Diet conversion — Seed → pellets and chop, without starving
• Taming plan — From cage-corner to step-up
• Screaming, biting, plucking — Find the function of the behaviour
• Foraging & toys — DIY enrichment from household stuff
• Bird-safe home audit — Fumes, cookware, plants, windows

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
