# Eighty-Eight

**Category:** Music & Audio  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.eightyeight`  
**Short description (≤80):** Piano practice that cracks the hard bars

## Full description
Eighty-Eight plans practice for the piece in front of you, untangles hard passages hands-separately, drills sight-reading and technique at the right tempo, and explains the theory hiding in your sheet music.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Practice plan — Blocks, tempos and hands
• Crack a passage — Photo or description → a 10-minute drill
• Sight-reading drill — 10 minutes a day that add up
• Explain this music — What is that chord and why is it there?
• Scales & technique — A 2-week technique block
