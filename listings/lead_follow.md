# Lead & Follow

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.leadfollow`  
**Short description (≤80):** Social dance moves, counts and confidence

## Full description
Lead & Follow helps social dancers learn salsa, bachata, swing, tango and ballroom: move breakdowns in counts, floor-craft for busy socials, partner communication and choreography for a wedding first dance.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Break down a move — Counts, feet, frame, connection
• Survive a social — Asking, blanking, crowds
• First-dance choreography — Song → moves, counts, 6-week plan
• Hear the music — Find the 1, hit the breaks
• Say it kindly — Feedback for a dance partner

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
