# Ride to Work

**Category:** Health & Fitness  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.ridetowork`  
**Short description (≤80):** Start cycling to work and keep doing it

## Full description
Ride to Work gets you commuting by bike: a build-up plan for your distance and fitness, what to wear for today's weather, the right bike and lock setup, route-choosing rules, and the real savings versus driving or transit.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• First four weeks — Build up days per week without burning out
• What do I wear today? — Weather → layers, gloves, visibility
• Choose my commuter — Bike, e-bike, folding or fix what you have
• Route & security rules — Pick a route, lock it, don't lose it
• What I'll save — Bike vs car vs transit, honestly

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
