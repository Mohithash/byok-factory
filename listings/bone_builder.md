## Full description
Bone Builder helps people over 50 protect bone density with safe impact and resistance exercise, calcium-and-vitamin-D meal plans, plain-English DEXA explanations and a guide to lifting and bending without risking the spine.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Bone-loading plan — Impact + resistance, safely
• Calcium day — Hit targets with real food
• Understand my DEXA — Photo or numbers → plain English
• Lift and bend safely — Everyday moves that spare the spine
• Fracture risk audit — Lifestyle factors you can change
