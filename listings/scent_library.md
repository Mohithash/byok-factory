# Scent Library

**Category:** Beauty  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.scentlibrary`  
**Short description (≤80):** Find, decode and wear fragrance like you mean it

## Full description
Scent Library recommends fragrances from what you already love, decodes any bottle from a photo, organises a scent wardrobe by season and occasion, and teaches layering and longevity.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Find my next scent — Loves + occasion → shortlist
• Decode a bottle — Photo → what it is and who it suits
• Scent wardrobe — The right bottle for the season and moment
• Layering lab — Combine what you own
• Make it last — Longevity for your skin and climate

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
