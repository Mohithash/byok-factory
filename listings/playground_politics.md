# Playground Politics

**Category:** Parenting

## Full description
Playground Politics helps parents of 5–12s through friendship fallouts, bullying at school, party exclusions and playdate nerves, with scripts for kids, letters for teachers and social-skills practice at home.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Conflict or bullying? — What's really going on
• Coach my child — Words for tomorrow
• Escalate to school — Letter, meeting, follow-up
• Party & playdate — Exclusions, invites, nerves
• Social skills practice — Games that teach it
