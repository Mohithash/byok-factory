# Honeymoon Atlas

**Category:** Travel & Local  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.honeymoonatlas`  
**Short description (≤80):** The trip after the wedding, planned like a gift

## Full description
Honeymoon Atlas picks the destination for your month and budget, builds a two-week itinerary with the splurge moments in the right places, plans surprises for your partner and sets a booking timeline that works around the wedding.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Pick the destination — Right place for your month and money
• Two-week itinerary — Slow days, big moments, in the right order
• Splurge vs save — Where the money actually shows
• Plan a surprise — One moment they'll talk about for years
• Booking timeline — What to book, when, around the wedding

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
