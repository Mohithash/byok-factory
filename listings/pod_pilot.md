# Pod Pilot

**Category:** Tools  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.podpilot`  
**Short description (≤80):** Manifests, crashing pods and pipelines, sorted

## Full description
Pod Pilot writes Kubernetes manifests with sane limits and probes, diagnoses crashing or pending pods from kubectl output, slims Dockerfiles, drafts CI pipelines and writes on-call runbooks.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Write a manifest — Service → Deployment, Service, Ingress
• Why is my pod broken? — kubectl describe / logs → cause
• Slim my Dockerfile — Smaller, faster, safer image
• Draft a CI pipeline — Build, test, scan, deploy
• Write a runbook — Alert → what on-call does at 3am

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
