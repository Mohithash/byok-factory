# Roll Log

**Category:** Sports  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.rolllog`  
**Short description (≤80):** Turn every BJJ roll into what to drill next

## Full description
Roll Log turns your Brazilian jiu-jitsu training notes into a game: it debriefs rolls, builds position-specific game plans and drilling blocks, preps you for competition and maps what your next belt actually expects.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Debrief a roll — What happened → what to fix
• Position game plan — Three linked techniques from one spot
• Competition prep — Weeks out → match morning
• Next-belt map — What the next stripe or belt expects
• Sore or tweaked? — Train, modify or rest

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
