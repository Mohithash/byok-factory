# Camp Pitch

**Category:** Travel & Local  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.camppitch`  
**Short description (≤80):** Family car camping without the 3am regrets

## Full description
Camp Pitch plans family and group car-camping trips: packing lists matched to the forecast, where to pitch from a photo of the site, a setup order that beats the dark, rainy-day rescues and fire and wildlife briefings.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Pack for the trip — Forecast-matched checklist
• Where to pitch — Photo of the site → best spot
• Setup order — Arrive at 5, done before dark
• Rainy day rescue — Keep everyone sane under canvas
• Fire & wildlife rules — Campfire, food storage, encounters

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
