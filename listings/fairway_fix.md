# Fairway Fix

**Category:** Sports  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.fairwayfix`  
**Short description (≤80):** Fix the ball flight, play the smart shot, score lower

## Full description
Fairway Fix diagnoses swing faults from your ball flight, builds a club-distance chart, plans strategy hole by hole, fixes chipping and putting, and turns range time into practice that actually lowers your handicap.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Fix my ball flight — Club + flight → cause and drill
• Club distance chart — Carries, gaps, partial wedges
• Play this hole — Shot-by-shot strategy
• Short game & putting — Chips, pitches, bunkers, putts
• Range session — Practice that transfers to the course

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
