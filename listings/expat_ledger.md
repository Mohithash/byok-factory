# Expat Ledger

**Category:** Finance  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.expatledger`  
**Short description (≤80):** Money across borders, without losing it in between

## Full description
Expat Ledger decodes salary offers abroad into real take-home and cost of living, plans transfers between countries, sets up banking on arrival, and explains tax-residency basics in plain language.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Decode an offer — Gross → net → what's left
• Cost of living — Rent to groceries, honestly
• Move money home — Cheapest route, right timing
• Banking setup — Open accounts without a local history
• Tax residency basics — Where do I actually owe tax?

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
