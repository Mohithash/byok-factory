# Living Room Gym

**Category:** Health & Fitness  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.livingroomgym`  
**Short description (≤80):** Real workouts in a small flat, no kit, no noise

## Full description
Living Room Gym writes no-equipment workouts that fit a rug-sized space and quiet hours, turns backpacks and chairs into weights, and progresses you for weeks without buying anything.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Today's workout — Minutes, goal, go
• Make weights — Backpacks, bottles, chairs into a loaded session
• Silent cardio — Out of breath without a single jump
• 4-week progression — Get stronger without buying anything
• Scan my room — Photo of the room into where and how to train

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
