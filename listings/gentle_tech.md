# Gentle Tech

**Category:** Tools  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.gentletech`  
**Short description (≤80):** Plain-English help with your phone, tablet and TV

## Full description
Gentle Tech explains what's on your screen, walks you through phone and tablet tasks one tap at a time, and checks suspicious messages before you reply.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• What is this screen? — Photo → plain-English explanation
• Show me how — Any task, one tap at a time
• Is this a scam? — Paste a message, email or pop-up
• Decode the jargon — Cloud? Wi-Fi? Two-factor?
• Set up a new device — Big text, easy eyes, safe

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
