# Theory Lane

**Category:** Auto & Vehicles  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.theorylane`  
**Short description (≤80):** Pass your driving theory test: signs, rules and hazards

## Full description
Theory Lane drills driving theory questions in your country's format, identifies road signs from a photo, coaches hazard perception and explains the rules of the road with the numbers examiners love to ask.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Mock test — Local format, local pass mark
• Identify a sign — Photo → meaning, action, look-alikes
• Hazard perception — Spot developing hazards earlier
• Explain a rule — The rule, the numbers, the exam phrasing
• Numbers cheat sheet — Every distance, limit and validity in one table

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
