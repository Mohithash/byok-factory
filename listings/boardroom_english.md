# Boardroom English

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.boardroomenglish`  
**Short description (≤80):** Sound fluent and professional in every meeting

## Full description
Boardroom English helps non-native professionals rewrite blunt or translated sentences, master meeting language, decode idioms and hedging, and rehearse presentations.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Say it professionally — Blunt or translated → natural
• Meeting phrasebook — Interrupt, disagree, push back politely
• What did they mean? — Idioms, hedging, corporate-speak
• Presentation rehearsal — Script, signposts, likely questions
• Small talk & networking — Before the meeting starts

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
