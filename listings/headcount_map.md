# Headcount Map

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.headcountmap`  
**Short description (≤80):** Hire the right roles in the right order for your runway

## Full description
Headcount Map turns your roadmap and runway into a sequenced hiring plan, writes role scorecards, suggests compensation bands, builds 30/60/90 onboarding plans and designs the org you are growing into.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Hiring plan — Who to hire, in what order, when
• Role scorecard — Mission, outcomes, competencies
• Comp band — Indicative base, bonus, equity
• 30/60/90 plan — Set a new hire up to win
• Org design — The structure you're growing into

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
