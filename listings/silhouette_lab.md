# Silhouette Lab

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.silhouettelab`  
**Short description (≤80):** Clothes that flatter your shape, whatever it is

## Full description
Silhouette Lab works out your body shape from four measurements, then tells you which necklines, rises, hems and fabrics balance your proportions, with a body-positive approach that talks about emphasis, never hiding.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Find my shape — 4 measurements → your proportions
• Will this suit me? — Photo of a piece → verdict and styling
• Jeans finder — Rise, leg and inseam for your shape
• Dress for the occasion — Silhouettes for the event
• Proportion play — Look taller, longer, balanced

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
