## Full description
Fuel Up plans daily nutrition for your training load, carb-loads before races, builds in-race fuelling and fixes GI issues.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Today's fuel — By session
• Carb-load — Race week
• In-race fuelling — Gels, drinks, real food
• Fix GI issues — Cramps, nausea

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
