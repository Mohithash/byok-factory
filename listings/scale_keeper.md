# Scale Keeper

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.scalekeeper`  
**Short description (≤80):** Heat, light and feeding done right for reptiles

## Full description
Scale Keeper designs species-correct enclosures with temperature gradients and UVB, builds feeding and supplement schedules, checks sheds and health from a photo, and preps you for the exotics vet.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Enclosure blueprint — Temps, UVB, substrate, hides
• Feeding schedule — Prey, portions, dusting
• Shed & health check — Photo → what to look at
• Why is it doing that? — Glass surfing, hiding, not basking
• Exotics vet prep — Turn observations into a clear history

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
