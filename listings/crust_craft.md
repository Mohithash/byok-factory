# Crust Craft

**Category:** Food & Drink  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.crustcraft`  
**Short description (≤80):** Pies and tarts with crisp bottoms and clean slices

## Full description
Crust Craft picks the right pastry for the pie, calculates fruit-filling thickener for clean slices, plans lattice and decorative tops, and diagnoses soggy bottoms and shrinking shells from a photo.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Pick the pastry — Right dough for the filling
• Thicken the filling — Starch grams per fruit and tin
• Design the top — Lattice, cut-outs, braids
• Soggy? Shrunk? Cracked? — Photo of a slice or shell
