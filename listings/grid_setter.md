# Grid Setter

**Category:** Games  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.gridsetter`  
**Short description (≤80):** Crossword clues that are fair, funny and fiendish

## Full description
Grid Setter is a crossword constructor's assistant: cryptic and quick clues for any answer, themed entry sets that fit your grid, editor-grade fairness checks on your clues and plain-English parses of any cryptic.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Clue this word — Several devices, ranked
• Theme a puzzle — Entries + a gimmick
• Check my clue — Editor's verdict
• Explain a cryptic — How does this clue work?
• Words for a pattern — Fit letters and a flavour

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
