# Iron Bell

**Category:** Health & Fitness  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.kettlecoach`  
**Short description (≤80):** Kettlebell sessions built around the bells you own

## Full description
Iron Bell programmes kettlebell training around the exact bells you own: swings, get-ups, presses, snatches and complexes, with form checks, a path to the next bell size and honest session reviews.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Today's session — Built from your bells and minutes
• Check my form — Photo or description of a swing, press or get-up
• Build a complex — Chains and flows without putting the bell down
• Earn the next bell — Bridge the gap to a heavier kettlebell
• Review my week — Paste your training log

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
