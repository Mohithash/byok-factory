# Miles Apart

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.milesapart`  
**Short description (≤80):** Long-distance love that feels close

## Full description
Miles Apart keeps long-distance couples connected: finds the hours you're both awake, plans virtual dates and visits worth the airfare, and maps the path to living in the same place.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Find our hours — When we're both awake and free
• Plan a virtual date — Better than 'so how was your day'
• Make the visit count — Plan the days you actually get
• We're drifting — When calls feel like a chore
• Close the distance — The plan to end up in one place

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
