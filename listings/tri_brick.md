# Brick Builder

**Category:** Sports  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.tribrick`  
**Short description (≤80):** Three sports, one week, no missed sessions

## Full description
Brick Builder fits swim, bike and run into your real week, writes brick sessions, sets up your transition and times your race day from alarm to finish line.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Plan my week — All three sports around your life
• Build a brick — Bike → run without jelly legs
• Transition setup — Photo of your kit → lay it out
• Race-day timeline — Alarm to finish line
• Taper me — Last 2 weeks, fresh not flat

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
