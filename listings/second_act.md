# Second Act

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.secondact`  
**Short description (≤80):** Design a retirement you'll actually enjoy

## Full description
Second Act helps new and soon-to-be retirees build a weekly rhythm, find hobbies and purpose, plan the trips and projects on the list, and handle the identity shift that nobody warns you about.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Design my week — Structure without a boss
• Find a new hobby — Matched to hands, budget and energy
• Purpose after work — Volunteering, mentoring, projects
• Plan a big one — Trip, project or milestone
• Who am I now? — The shift nobody warns you about

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
