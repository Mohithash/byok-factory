# Time Machine

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.historytime`  
**Short description (≤80):** History explained like a great documentary

## Full description
Time Machine explains any period, event or person with context, timelines, primary-source flavour and 'what if' debates.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Explain an event — Causes, course, consequences
• A life in brief — Any historical figure
• Compare — Two things side by side
• Debate a 'what if' — Counterfactual, carefully

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
