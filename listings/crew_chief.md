# Crew Chief

**Category:** Sports  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.crewchief`  
**Short description (≤80):** Ultra race-day logistics for runners and their crews

## Full description
Crew Chief runs the logistics of an ultra: aid-station split sheets, crew and pacer plans, drop-bag packing, sleep strategy for 100+ miles and a playbook for when things go wrong.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Split sheet — Aid station arrival windows
• Crew & pacer plan — Who does what, where, when
• Drop bags — What goes in which bag
• Sleep & night strategy — 100 miles and beyond
• Something's wrong — Nausea, blisters, lows

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
