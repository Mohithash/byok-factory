## Full description
Whisk & Bowl decodes matcha grades from a tin photo, coaches usucha and koicha technique, builds lattes and bakes, and plans caffeine so the afternoon bowl doesn't wreck your sleep.

WHAT'S INSIDE

• Whisk technique — Usucha, koicha, or with your kit
• Latte & drinks — Iced, hot, strawberry, hojicha swap
• Bake with matcha — Cookies, cakes, mochi
• Caffeine plan — Bowls, lattes and sleep
