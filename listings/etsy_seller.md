# Maker Shop

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.etsyseller`  
**Short description (≤80):** Sell your handmade goods online

## Full description
Maker Shop writes listings with SEO-friendly titles, prices your products properly, plans photos and handles customer messages.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Write a listing — Title, description, tags
• Price a product — Materials, time, fees
• Photo plan — Phone-friendly
• Customer message — Reply well

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
