# Bake Sale Boss

**Category:** Food & Drink  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.bakesaleboss`  
**Short description (≤80):** Price, plan and sell your home bakes

## Full description
Bake Sale Boss prices every bake from ingredients, time and overheads, plans market menus and order books, drafts allergen labels and writes the quotes and posts that win customers.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Price a bake — Ingredients + time + overhead → price
• Plan a market — What to bake, how much, what to charge
• Allergen label — Ingredient and allergen declaration
• Customer quote & reply — Answer an enquiry with a clear quote
• Sell it online — Posts and captions for a bake

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
