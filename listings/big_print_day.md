# Big Print Planner

**Category:** Productivity  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.bigprintday`  
**Short description (≤80):** Plans, letters and lists you can actually read

## Full description
Big Print Planner is built for low vision: it turns any letter or bill into a short plain summary, plans your day as a few clear steps, and rewrites recipes, lists and instructions so they work with large text or a screen reader.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Read this for me — Photo of a letter, bill or label
• Plan my day — A few clear steps
• Big-print recipe — Any recipe, made readable
• Make a room easier to see — Contrast, light and labels
• Shopping list — Grouped, short, in order

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
