# Van Dwell

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.vandwell`  
**Short description (≤80):** Power, parking, admin and the weekly rhythm of van life

## Full description
Van Dwell is for people already living or weekending in a van: ration today's power from the battery and the forecast, judge an overnight spot, keep a weekly water-waste-laundry-wifi rhythm, sort mail, domicile and insurance as a nomad, and run a pre-trip inspection before a long drive.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Today's power budget — Battery + forecast → what to run
• Nomad admin — Mail, domicile, insurance, healthcare
• Check an overnight spot — Stay or move on?
• Weekly rhythm — Water, waste, laundry, wifi, body
• Pre-trip inspection — Before a long drive

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
