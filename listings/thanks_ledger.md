# Thanks Ledger

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.thanksledger`  
**Short description (≤80):** A gratitude journal that notices more than you do

## Full description
Thanks Ledger turns a messy day into three specific things worth keeping, spots what keeps showing up across your entries, and writes the thank-you notes you've been meaning to send.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Tonight's three — Turn your day into three keepers
• Write a thank-you — A note someone will keep
• Weekly rewind — Paste entries → what keeps showing up
• Savour it — Stretch a good moment
• 30-day prompt jar — A month of prompts

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
