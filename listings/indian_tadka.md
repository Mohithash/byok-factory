# Tadka Kitchen

**Category:** Food & Drink

## Full description
Tadka Kitchen teaches real regional Indian home cooking: curries and dals from what you have, a masala dabba built for how you cook, thali planning, flatbread coaching and fixes for a curry that went wrong.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Cook a curry or dal — Regional dish from your main ingredient
• Build my masala dabba — The 7 spices for your cooking + the next 10
• Plan a thali — A balanced plate for guests or a Sunday
• Fix my curry — Bland, watery, raw-spice or too hot
• Flatbread coach — Roti, naan, paratha, dosa, bhatura
