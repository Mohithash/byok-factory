# Retro Room

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.retroroom`  
**Short description (≤80):** Team rituals people don't dread

## Full description
Retro Room designs team rituals that people actually show up for: retrospectives matched to the team's mood, standups that stay short, working agreements everyone owns and recognition that feels real.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Design a retro — A format for how the team feels
• Theme the sticky notes — Raw notes → themes, actions, summary
• Working agreements — 'We…' statements the team will own
• Fix our standup — Shorter, peer-to-peer, remote-friendly
• Celebrate a win — Recognition that names real contributions

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
