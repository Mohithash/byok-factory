# Fertile Signs

**Category:** Health & Fitness  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.fertilesigns`  
**Short description (≤80):** Read your fertility signs with confidence

## Full description
Fertile Signs interprets your basal body temperature, cervical mucus and OPK results using sympto-thermal rules, confirms ovulation, explains confusing cycles and teaches each sign step by step.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Read my chart — Paste temps + mucus + OPKs
• Read my OPK — Photo of the strip
• Where am I today? — Fertile or not, from what you've seen
• Learn a sign — Mucus, temperature, cervix, OPKs
• Weird cycle — Delayed ovulation, split peaks, no shift

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
