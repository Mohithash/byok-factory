# Focus Sprint

**Category:** Productivity  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.focussprint`  
**Short description (≤80):** Beat procrastination one sprint at a time

## Full description
Focus Sprint breaks scary tasks into 25-minute sprints, writes your first tiny step, and runs a quick review so tomorrow starts easier.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Plan sprints — One scary task → sprints
• I'm stuck — Get unstuck now
• Plan my day — Tasks → a realistic schedule
• End-of-day review — 2 minutes, honest

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
