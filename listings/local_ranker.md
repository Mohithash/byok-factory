# Local Ranker

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.localranker`  
**Short description (≤80):** Get found on Maps and win the local search

## Full description
Local Ranker optimises your Google Business Profile, writes review replies and review-request messages, plans local content and prepares consistent listings for every directory.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Optimise my profile — Google Business Profile audit
• Reply to reviews — Paste reviews → replies in your voice
• Get more reviews — Messages that politely ask
• Local content plan — Posts, pages and events for your area
• Citations & listings — Consistent NAP everywhere

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
