# Haggle

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.negotiator`  
**Short description (≤80):** Scripts for every negotiation

## Full description
Haggle prepares you for salary talks, rent, car deals, freelance rates and refunds with numbers, scripts and counters.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Prep sheet — Any negotiation
• Give me a script — What to actually say
• Negotiation email — Counter in writing
• Get a refund — Consumer complaint that works

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
