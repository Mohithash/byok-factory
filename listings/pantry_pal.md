# Pantry Pal

**Category:** Food & Drink  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.pantrypal`  
**Short description (≤80):** Dinner from what's already in your kitchen

## Full description
Pantry Pal turns whatever is in your fridge into real dinner ideas, shopping lists and swaps. Bring your own AI key; nothing is stored anywhere but your phone.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• What can I cook? — Recipes that use what you have
• Scan my fridge — Photo → inventory + ideas
• Swap an ingredient — Missing something? Fix it
• Weekly plan — Dinners + one shopping list
• Rescue leftovers — Turn last night into tonight

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
