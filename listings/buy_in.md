# Buy-In

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.buyin`  
**Short description (≤80):** Cross-team support for ideas you can't mandate

## Full description
Buy-In helps people who lead without authority — product managers, programme leads, chiefs of staff — map who matters, sequence the conversations, pitch a sceptical peer, write the one-page proposal and recover from a no.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Map the stakeholders — Power, interest, stance — and who moves whom
• Sequence the conversations — Who to talk to, in what order, saying what
• Pitch a sceptical peer — One conversation, one convert
• Write the proposal — One page they can say yes to
• They said no — Understand the no and find the next move

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
