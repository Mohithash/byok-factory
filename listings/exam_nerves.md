# Exam Nerves

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.examnerves`  
**Short description (≤80):** Calm, focused and rested on test day

## Full description
Exam Nerves builds a test-week routine for sleep, food and light revision, gives in-exam reset techniques for blanking and panic, rewrites the thoughts that spiral, and debriefs bad scores without the doom.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Test-week plan — Taper, sleep and the night before
• In-exam reset — 60 seconds, silent, at your desk
• Rewrite the spiral — Catch the thought, balance it
• Debrief a bad score — Separate prep, execution and luck
• Sleep & fuel for test day — Hour-by-hour from waking to the last question

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
