# City Break

**Category:** Travel & Local  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.citybreak`  
**Short description (≤80):** 48 hours in any city, done right

## Full description
City Break plans weekend itineraries grouped by neighbourhood, finds food worth the walk, and handles logistics from the airport to the last train.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Plan 48 hours — City + dates
• Where to eat — Real, not touristy
• Rainy-day plan — Indoors, still great
• Local basics — Phrases, tipping, customs

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
