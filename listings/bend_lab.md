# Bend Lab

**Category:** Health & Fitness  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.bendlab`  
**Short description (≤80):** Front splits, bridge, pancake, trained like a skill

## Full description
Bend Lab trains flexibility goals the way gymnasts do: measured tests, an 8-week programme for splits, bridge, pancake or pike, PNF and loaded stretching coached step by step, and retests that show real centimetres.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Measure my baseline — Tests with a tape measure and a wall
• 8-week programme — Skill-style flexibility training
• Today's session — One flexibility session, coached
• Check a stretch — Photo in the position
• Retest and adjust — Compare with baseline, change the plan

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
