# Integrated Edge

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.integratededge`  
**Short description (≤80):** TOEFL iBT integrated tasks, note-taking and timed speaking

## Full description
Integrated Edge prepares you for the TOEFL iBT: it builds integrated writing and speaking sets from reading-plus-lecture pairs, times your 45-second responses, trains a note-taking system that keeps up, and scores your academic-discussion posts.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Integrated writing set — Reading + lecture that disagree, with a template
• Speaking task drill — Prompt, template, model, and a score if you paste yours
• Note-taking system — Symbols, layout and a worked lecture
• Academic discussion post — Plan and model a 10-minute post, or score yours
• Study plan to test day — Weekly plan by section, plus the final 48 hours

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
