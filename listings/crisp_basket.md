# Crisp Basket

**Category:** Food & Drink

## Full description
Crisp Basket converts any oven recipe to your exact air fryer, gives times for frozen and fresh foods, plans multi-item baskets that finish together, and fixes smoke, sticking and soggy results.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Convert a recipe — Oven → air fryer
• Cook this — Photo or name → settings
• Basket plan — Several things, one dinner, done together
• Air-fryer week — 5 dinners, mostly one basket
• Fix it — Smoke, sticking, soggy or raw inside
