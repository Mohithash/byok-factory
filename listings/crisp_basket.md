# Crisp Basket

**Category:** Food & Drink  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.crispbasket`  
**Short description (≤80):** Air-fryer times, conversions and dinners that crunch

## Full description
Crisp Basket converts any oven recipe to your exact air fryer, gives times for frozen and fresh foods, plans multi-item baskets that finish together, and fixes smoke, sticking and soggy results.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Convert a recipe — Oven → air fryer
• Cook this — Photo or name → settings
• Basket plan — Several things, one dinner, done together
• Air-fryer week — 5 dinners, mostly one basket
• Fix it — Smoke, sticking, soggy or raw inside

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
