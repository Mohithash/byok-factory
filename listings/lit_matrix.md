# Lit Matrix

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.litmatrix`  
**Short description (≤80):** A literature review that argues, not a list of summaries

## Full description
Lit Matrix builds database search strings, logs each paper into a synthesis matrix, groups them into themes, finds the gap your study fills and turns your notes into synthesis paragraphs that compare rather than list.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Search strings — Boolean strings per database
• Add a paper — Abstract or notes → matrix row
• Find the themes — Many papers → themes and the gap
• Appraise a study — Is it strong enough to lean on?
• Synthesis paragraph — Your notes on a theme → one argued paragraph

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
