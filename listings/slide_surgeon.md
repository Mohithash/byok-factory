# Slide Surgeon

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.slidesurgeon`  
**Short description (≤80):** Business decks that make one point per slide

## Full description
Slide Surgeon builds the storyline before the slides, rewrites titles into takeaways, critiques a chart slide from a photo, cuts a long deck down to the executive version and writes the speaker notes.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Build the storyline — SCQA → pyramid → slide skeleton
• Rewrite my titles — Topic labels → action titles
• Fix this chart — Photo of a data slide → what to change
• Cut to the exec version — 40 slides → 5 for leadership
• Speaker notes — What to say on each slide

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
