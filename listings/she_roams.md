# She Roams

**Category:** Travel & Local  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.sheroams`  
**Short description (≤80):** Solo female travel with confidence, not fear

## Full description
She Roams is a planning companion for women travelling alone: destination briefings that are honest rather than alarmist, a first-night arrival plan, scripts for unwanted attention, safe ways to meet people, and a check-in system for the people back home.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Destination briefing — What it's really like for women alone
• First-night plan — From landing to locked door
• Unwanted attention — Words that work, exits that work
• Meet people safely — Company without the risk
• Check-in system — Keep home informed, not worried

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
