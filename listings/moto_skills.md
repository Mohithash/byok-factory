# Lean Angle

**Category:** Auto & Vehicles  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.motoskills`  
**Short description (≤80):** Ride smoother, corner with confidence

## Full description
Lean Angle coaches new and returning motorcyclists with car-park drills, cornering and braking analysis, a gear kit for your budget, honest near-miss debriefs and briefings for leading group rides.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Car-park session — A full session of drills
• Corner coaching — Describe the bend, fix the line
• Gear kit — Head to toe for your budget
• Near-miss debrief — Turn a scare into a lesson
• Group ride briefing — Lead a ride without drama
