# Shelf Sense

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.shelfsense`  
**Short description (≤80):** Stock the right amount of the right things

## Full description
Shelf Sense helps independent shop owners set reorder points, plan open-to-buy budgets, clear slow stock without killing margin and tidy displays from a photo.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Reorder points — When and how much to reorder
• Open-to-buy plan — A buying budget for the season
• Clear slow stock — Markdown ladder that frees cash
• Shelf check — Photo of a display → merchandising fixes
• Supplier order email — A clean purchase order to send

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
