# Error Bars

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.errorbars`  
**Short description (≤80):** Lab reports with proper uncertainty, fits and discussion

## Full description
Error Bars propagates uncertainties, fits your data with a slope and its error, marks your graph from a photo, critiques your method for systematic errors and drafts the discussion section markers actually reward.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Propagate uncertainty — Formula + measurements → result ± error
• Fit my data — x, y → slope, intercept, uncertainties
• Check my graph — Photo → what a marker would say
• Sources of error — Random vs systematic, with fixes
• Draft the discussion — Results → conclusion and evaluation
