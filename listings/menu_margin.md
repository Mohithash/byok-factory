# Menu Margins

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.menumargin`  
**Short description (≤80):** Know what every dish really earns

## Full description
Menu Margins costs every dish from its recipe, sorts your menu into stars and dogs, reprices for your target food cost and rewrites descriptions that sell.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Cost a dish — Recipe + prices → plate cost and margin
• Menu engineering — Stars, plowhorses, puzzles, dogs
• Reprice the menu — New prices with the psychology built in
• Critique my menu — Photo of the menu → layout and copy fixes
• Price a special — Tonight's dish, priced and pitched
