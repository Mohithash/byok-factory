# Ethos Invest

**Category:** Finance  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.ethosinvest`  
**Short description (≤80):** Invest in line with your values, without the jargon

## Full description
Ethos Invest helps beginners invest by their values: a values screen you can actually apply, greenwash checks on fund marketing, a test of whether a fund's holdings pass your screen, a plan for switching your bank and pension, and plain-English answers to the hard questions.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Build my values screen — Exclusions, inclusions, dealbreakers
• Greenwash check — Photo or paste → what it really commits to
• Does it pass my screen? — Top holdings → what clashes with your values
• Move my bank & pension — Where your money already sits
• Hard questions — Does it even work? Do I lose returns?

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
