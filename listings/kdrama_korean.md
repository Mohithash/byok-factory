# Drama Hangul

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.kdramakorean`  
**Short description (≤80):** Learn Korean from the K-dramas you already binge

## Full description
Drama Hangul turns your favourite K-dramas into Korean lessons: scene breakdowns, speech-level explanations, genre phrase packs and Hangul reading from subtitles and screenshots.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Break down a scene — Paste the subtitle lines
• Banmal or jondaetmal? — Same sentence, every speech level
• Phrase pack — Vocab for a genre or trope
• Read this screenshot — Hangul from captions, signs, texts
• Binge study plan — Learn while you watch this week

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
