# Reading Bridge

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.readingbridge`  
**Short description (≤80):** Dyslexia-friendly reading practice, ten minutes a night

## Full description
Reading Bridge gives parents of dyslexic and struggling readers structured-literacy practice at home: phonics-pattern drills, decodable stories, book picks that match reading level and real interests, and assistive-tech setup.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Tonight's drill — One pattern, multisensory
• Decodable story — Only patterns they know
• Book picks — Right level, real interest
• Explain it to my child — Dyslexia, in their words
• Assistive tech setup — Text-to-speech, dictation, fonts

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
