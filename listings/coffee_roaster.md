# Roast Curve

**Category:** Food & Drink  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.coffeeroaster`  
**Short description (≤80):** Roast green coffee at home, batch by batch

## Full description
Roast Curve plans roast profiles for your roaster and beans, analyses your roast logs by first-crack timing, judges roast colour from a photo, and schedules resting so you brew at peak.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Plan a roast profile — Beans → target curve
• Analyse my roast log — Paste times and temps
• Judge the roast — Photo of beans → level and defects
• Buy green beans — What to order next
• Rest & brew schedule — When each batch peaks

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
