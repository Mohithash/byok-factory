## Full description
Meno Strong builds strength and bone-loading programmes, protein-first meal plans and joint-friendly cardio for the perimenopause years, then explains your DEXA and blood results in plain English.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Strength block — 4 weeks, progressive
• Protein-first day — Hit 1.2–1.6 g/kg without a spreadsheet
• Joint-friendly cardio — Zone 2 + short intervals, no injuries
• Decode my results — DEXA, cholesterol, HbA1c, vitamin D
• Midlife body changes — Weight, belly, plateaus, without crash diets
