# Small Wonder

**Category:** House & Home  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.smallwonder`  
**Short description (≤80):** Make a tiny flat live large

## Full description
Small Wonder zones studios and tiny flats, checks whether furniture fits to the centimetre, finds storage you are not using and makes hosting possible in 30 square metres.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Zone my space — Separate sleep, work and eat in one room
• Will it fit? — Check a piece against your space and stairs
• Dual-purpose furniture — Pieces that do two jobs
• Storage I'm not using — Photo → walls, doors, ceiling, under-bed
• Host in a tiny flat — Dinner or overnight guests without panic

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
