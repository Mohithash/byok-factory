# Exam Countdown

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.studyplanner`  
**Short description (≤80):** A revision timetable that adapts to you

## Full description
Exam Countdown turns your subjects and exam dates into a spaced-repetition revision timetable, then builds active-recall sessions and past-paper strategies.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Build timetable — From today to the exams
• Study session — 45 minutes, one topic
• Past-paper strategy — How to attack the exam
• I'm behind — Rescue plan

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.

## Tools
- 📅 **Build timetable** — From today to the exams
- ⏱️ **Study session** — 45 minutes, one topic
- 📝 **Past-paper strategy** — How to attack the exam
- 🆘 **I'm behind** — Rescue plan
