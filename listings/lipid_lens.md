# Lipid Lens

**Category:** Medical  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.lipidlens`  
**Short description (≤80):** Cholesterol numbers explained, and a plan to move them

## Full description
Lipid Lens explains your cholesterol panel, scans labels for saturated fat, builds 12-week eating and movement plans, and helps you prepare for the statin conversation with your doctor.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Explain my panel — LDL, HDL, triglycerides, ratios
• Saturated-fat label scan — Photo → verdict
• 12-week plan — Food and movement that move LDL
• The statin conversation — Weigh it up with your doctor
• Eating out — Order well anywhere

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
