# Brevet Card

**Category:** Sports  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.brevetcard`  
**Short description (≤80):** Audax and ultra-distance rides, timed to the control

## Full description
Brevet Card plans randonnées and ultra-distance cycling events from 200 km to 1,200 km: control deadlines, sleep stops, night-riding kit and a strategy for when you drift behind the clock.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Time-budget the controls — Deadlines, buffers, stop lengths
• Sleep strategy — Where and how long to sleep
• Night-ride kit check — Photo of your lights & layers
• I'm behind the clock — Rescue the ride
• Step up a distance — From your longest ride to the next

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
