# Baseline

**Category:** Sports  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.baselinetennis`  
**Short description (≤80):** Stroke fixes, serve clinics and match tactics

## Full description
Baseline fixes your tennis strokes and serve one cue at a time, builds tactics against the opponent in front of you, plans practice with a partner, wall or machine, and turns match notes into a clear next step.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Fix a stroke — Symptom → cause, drill, cue
• Serve clinic — Toss, contact, targets, ball counts
• Beat this opponent — Scout → game plan
• Practice plan — Partner, wall, machine or feed
• Review my match — Notes → the stat that decided it

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
