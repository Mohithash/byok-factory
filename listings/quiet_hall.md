# Quiet Hall

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.quiethall`  
**Short description (≤80):** Exam nerves handled: the night before, in the room, after

## Full description
Quiet Hall is a pocket toolkit for exam anxiety: a night-before plan, a 60-second reset for panic in the room, a strategy for when your mind goes blank, a stop to the post-exam spiral, and help requesting the support you are entitled to.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Night-before plan — Sleep, bag, brain, all sorted
• Panic in the room — 60 seconds to get back to the paper
• Mind went blank — Retrieve it, or earn partial credit
• Post-exam spiral — Stop replaying it
• Ask for adjustments — Extra time, separate room, deferrals

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
