# Room Restyle

**Category:** House & Home  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.roomrestyle`  
**Short description (≤80):** Redesign any room from one photo

## Full description
Room Restyle critiques a room from a photo, names the style you are reaching for, builds a palette and layout, and turns it into a shopping list that fits your budget.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Critique my room — Photo → what's working, what's not
• Build a palette — Walls, textiles and accents that agree
• Plan the layout — Furniture placement from your dimensions
• Shopping list — Everything to buy, to a budget
• Name my style — From what you like to a clear direction

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
