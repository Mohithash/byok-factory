# Freezer Stash

**Category:** Food & Drink  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.freezerstash`  
**Short description (≤80):** Cook double, freeze half, never waste dinner

## Full description
Freezer Stash tells you what freezes well and how, plans double-and-freeze cooking sessions, inventories your freezer from a photo, and schedules safe defrosting so tonight's dinner is ready in time.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Can I freeze this? — How, how long, how to bring it back
• Double-and-freeze session — Cook once, stock the freezer
• Inventory my freezer — Photo → what's in there
• Defrost for tonight — Safe timing that actually works
• Freezer burn & fails — Rescue it or bin it
