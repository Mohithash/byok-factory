# Step Ahead

**Category:** Parenting  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.stepahead`  
**Short description (≤80):** Find your footing as a stepparent

## Full description
Step Ahead helps stepparents and blended families find their role: how involved to be and when, discipline agreements with your partner, new-family rituals, and what to say to 'you're not my mum'.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• My role right now — Involved enough, not too much
• Discipline agreement — Same rules, agreed in private
• 'You're not my mum' — The line every stepparent hears
• New-family rituals — Traditions that belong to this household
• Loyalty binds — Kids caught between two homes

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
