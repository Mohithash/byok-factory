# First Lead

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.firstlead`  
**Short description (≤80):** The pocket coach for brand-new managers

## Full description
First Lead is the pocket coach for first-time managers: a first-90-days plan, a 'how I work' README, delegation that isn't dumping, managing former peers and a rescue plan for the week you're drowning.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• My first 90 days — Listen, decide, deliver — in that order
• My manager README — Tell the team how you work
• What should I delegate? — Keep, delegate, drop or automate
• Managing former peers — When your friend is now your report
• I'm drowning — Triage a week that's too much

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
