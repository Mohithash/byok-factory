# Term Purse

**Category:** Finance  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.termpurse`  
**Short description (≤80):** Make the loan drop last until the next one

## Full description
Term Purse turns each student loan or grant instalment into a weekly allowance that survives the whole term, plans around rent day and exam weeks, and finds the part-time hours that pay without wrecking your grades.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Stretch this instalment — Lump sum → weekly allowance
• Part-time hours math — How many shifts you actually need
• Course kit on a budget — Textbooks, software, equipment
• Money ran out — Get to the next drop safely
• Summer gap plan — No loan over the break

FEATURES
• Clear answers: steps, tickable checklists, tables, cards and key facts
• Follow-up questions in a thread that remembers the conversation
• Regenerate any answer, or edit your input and run it again
• Answers in 40+ languages, brief, standard or detailed
• Standing instructions applied to every answer (e.g. "metric units")
• Read answers aloud, or type with your voice
• Save as PDF, export Markdown, copy or share
• Share text or photos into the app from any other app
• Home-screen shortcuts straight to each tool
• Favourites, notes, search and filters for everything you save
• Backup & restore to a file you choose (never includes your key)
• Light, dark or system theme with Material You colours
• Usage counter for your requests and tokens
• Cancel any request; automatic retry when the provider is busy
• Pick any model from your provider's list

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
