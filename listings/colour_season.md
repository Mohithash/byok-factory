# Colour Season

**Category:** Beauty  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.colourseason`  
**Short description (≤80):** The colours that make you look awake and alive

## Full description
Colour Season works out your seasonal colour palette from a daylight photo, tells you whether a specific colour flatters you, builds a shopping palette with hex codes and matches makeup and hair colour to it.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Find my season — Daylight photo → season
• Does this colour suit me? — Colour name or photo → verdict
• Build my palette — Shopping palette with hex codes
• Makeup for my season — Lip, blush, brow, liner
• Hair colour for my season — Before you book the colourist

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
