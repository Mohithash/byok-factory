# Harf Arabic

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.harfarabic`  
**Short description (≤80):** Read Arabic script letter by letter, form by form

## Full description
Harf Arabic teaches the alphabet with all four letter forms, shows how letters join into words, decodes signs from photos and writes your name in Arabic.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Next letters — Four forms, sounds, joining rules
• Build a word — Watch letters connect
• Read a sign — Photo → letters, sounds, meaning
• Harakat and sukun — The marks that make words readable
• My name in Arabic — Spelling, forms, and how it sounds

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
