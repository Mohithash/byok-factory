# Lumen Plan

**Category:** House & Home  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.lumenplan`  
**Short description (≤80):** Lighting that makes every room feel right

## Full description
Lumen Plan designs layered lighting room by room: how many lumens, which colour temperature, where fixtures go, which bulbs to buy and how to fix a room that feels flat or harsh.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Light this room — Layers, lumens and kelvin
• Why does it feel wrong? — Photo of the room at night → diagnosis
• Which bulbs? — Decode fittings, lumens, kelvin, dimmable
• Evening scenes — Schedules for wake, work and wind-down
• Lighting shopping list — Fixtures and bulbs for the whole home

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
