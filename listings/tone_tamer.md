# Tone Tamer

**Category:** Education

## Full description
Tone Tamer teaches Mandarin's four tones and tricky pinyin sounds with paired drills, explains tone sandhi in any sentence, and reads menus and signs from photos with full pinyin.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Tone-pair drill — Two-syllable words, one pair at a time
• Tone changes in this sentence — Third-tone, 一 and 不 rules
• Pinyin traps — zh/ch/sh vs j/q/x, ü, -n vs -ng
• Read a menu or sign — Photo → characters, pinyin, meaning
• Say this sentence — Full pinyin, rhythm, tone marks
