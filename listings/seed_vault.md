# Seed Vault

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.seedvault`  
**Short description (≤80):** Save, store and swap seeds from what you grow

## Full description
Seed Vault teaches seed saving crop by crop, tests and tracks viability, catalogues your seed box from a photo with sow-by dates, and writes swap listings and labels so nothing goes stale in a drawer.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Save seed from… — Harvest, clean, dry, store — per crop
• Scan my seed box — Photo of packets → sow-by table
• Viability test — Paper-towel germination test
• Swap listing & labels — Write it up for a seed swap
• Plan from my box — Next season from what you already have

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
