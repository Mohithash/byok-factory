# Essay Coach

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.essaycoach`  
**Short description (≤80):** Outlines, feedback and thesis polish

## Full description
Essay Coach turns prompts into outlines, gives rubric-based feedback on drafts, sharpens thesis statements and fixes citations — without writing it for you.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Outline from a prompt — Structure before words
• Feedback on my draft — Paste a draft
• Sharpen my thesis — From vague to arguable
• Fix citations — Format references

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.

## Tools
- 🗂️ **Outline from a prompt** — Structure before words
- 📝 **Feedback on my draft** — Paste a draft
- 🎯 **Sharpen my thesis** — From vague to arguable
- 📚 **Fix citations** — Format references
