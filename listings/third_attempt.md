# Third Attempt

**Category:** Health & Fitness  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.thirdattempt`  
**Short description (≤80):** Programming and meet prep for squat, bench, deadlift

## Full description
Third Attempt writes powerlifting blocks from your current maxes, picks meet-day attempts and warm-up timing, fixes sticking points and plans a weight cut that will not cost you kilos on the platform.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Build a block — Percentage or RPE, 4–8 weeks
• Pick my attempts — Openers to thirds plus warm-ups
• Fix a sticking point — Where the lift fails and why
• Make weight — A weigh-in plan that keeps your strength
• Meet-day playbook — Gear, timing, food, headspace

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
