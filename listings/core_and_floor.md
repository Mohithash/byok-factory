# Core & Floor

**Category:** Health & Fitness  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.coreandfloor`  
**Short description (≤80):** Pelvic floor, core and bladder — rebuilt properly

## Full description
Core & Floor builds pelvic floor and deep-core programmes, analyses your bladder diary, guides a diastasis recti self-check, adapts exercise for leaks and prolapse, and preps you for a pelvic health physio visit.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Pelvic floor programme — 4 weeks, with cues that work
• Bladder diary — Paste 24–72h of drinks and toilet trips
• Diastasis check — Self-check + deep-core rehab
• Can I do this? — Exercise, lifting, running with symptoms
• Pelvic physio visit — What happens, what to ask

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
