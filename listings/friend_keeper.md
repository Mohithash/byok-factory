# Friend Keeper

**Category:** Social  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.friendkeeper`  
**Short description (≤80):** Keep the friendships that matter from fading

## Full description
Friend Keeper helps busy adults maintain real friendships: reconnect after months of silence, plan hangs that fit real life, handle a falling-out, and know when a friendship has run its course.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Reconnect — It's been months; break the silence
• Plan a hang — Fits both calendars, costs little
• We fell out — Say the hard thing without losing them
• Is it fading? — One-sided? Outgrown? Just busy?
• Say something real — Birthday, promotion, loss, big news

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
