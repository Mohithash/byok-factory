# Cavy Corner

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.cavycorner`  
**Short description (≤80):** Vitamin C, cage space and the language of wheeks

## Full description
Cavy Corner plans vitamin-C-rich veg menus, sizes C&C cages for your herd, bonds pairs and trios, translates wheeks and rumbles, and runs weekly weigh-ins and health checks.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Weekly veg menu — Vitamin C without bladder stones
• Cage calculator — Right-size a C&C for your herd
• Bond pigs — Intro a new pig or fix a fallout
• What did that mean? — Wheeks, rumbles, popcorns and teeth chatter
• Weekly health check — Weigh, feel, look — and when to worry

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
