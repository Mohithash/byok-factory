# Send Beta

**Category:** Sports  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.sendbeta`  
**Short description (≤80):** Project tactics and training that match your grade

## Full description
Send Beta is a coach for boulderers and sport climbers: it reads your project from a photo, writes training blocks matched to your grade, guides finger and elbow niggles, analyses your grade pyramid and preps trips.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Read my project — Photo → sequence and tactics
• Training block — Weeks, days, sets, intensity
• Finger or elbow niggle — What it might be, what to do
• Grade pyramid — Send log → what's missing
• Trip prep — Gear, days, skin, safety

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
