# Lease Abroad

**Category:** House & Home  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.leaseabroad`  
**Short description (≤80):** Rent a home in a new country without getting stung

## Full description
Lease Abroad explains how renting really works in your destination country, decodes a foreign-language lease from a photo, checks listings for scams, and gets your application past guarantor and credit-history hurdles.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• How renting works here — Deposits, fees, notice, what's normal
• Decode this lease — Photo of the contract → plain English
• Is this listing a scam? — Check before you pay anything
• Win the application — No local credit history? No guarantor?
• Viewing day — What to check, ask and photograph

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
