# Home Barista

**Category:** Food & Drink  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.baristahome`  
**Short description (≤80):** Dial in café-quality coffee at home

## Full description
Home Barista helps you dial in espresso, pour-over, AeroPress and cold brew with exact ratios, then troubleshoots taste problems.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Dial-in recipe — Ratio, time, grind for your method
• Fix my cup — Sour? Bitter? Weak?
• Choose beans — What to buy for how you brew
• Milk & latte art — Texture and pour basics

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
