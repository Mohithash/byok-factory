# Trade Brief

**Category:** House & Home  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.tradebrief`  
**Short description (≤80):** Hire, brief and manage tradespeople without the drama

## Full description
Trade Brief writes clear scopes of work, vets contractors before you hire, sets contract and payment terms, turns photos into snagging lists and scripts the hard conversations when work slips.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Write the scope — A spec every quote must answer
• Vet a contractor — References, insurance, warning signs
• Contract & payments — Milestones, retention, clauses
• Snag list from a photo — Finished work → defects and a message
• Handle a problem — Late, over budget, poor work, no-show

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
