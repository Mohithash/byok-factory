# Manners Map

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.mannersmap`  
**Short description (≤80):** Get the customs right before you get there

## Full description
Manners Map briefs you on the etiquette of any country or community — greetings, dining, gifts, business meetings, dress at sacred sites — and decodes awkward moments after they happen.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Country briefing — Greetings, dining, taboos
• Dining etiquette — Table manners for one meal
• Business meeting norms — Cards, hierarchy, small talk, follow-up
• Visiting a sacred site — Dress, behaviour, photos
• What just happened? — Decode an awkward moment

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
