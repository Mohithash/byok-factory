# Stable Mate

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.stablemate`  
**Short description (≤80):** Feed, rug, condition and care for your horse

## Full description
Stable Mate builds forage-first feed rations by weight and workload, picks the right rug for tonight's weather, plans fitness for your discipline, and triages colic, lameness and wounds with clear call-the-vet rules.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Feed ration — Forage-first, by weight and work
• Rug for tonight — Weather → rug weight
• Fitness plan — 6 weeks toward a goal
• Colic, lameness or wound — What to do right now
• Yard calendar — Vaccines, teeth, worming, farrier

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
