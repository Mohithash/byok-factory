# Legacy Binder

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.legacybinder`  
**Short description (≤80):** Get your affairs in order, kindly and clearly

## Full description
Legacy Binder helps you organise the documents, accounts and wishes your family would need, plan your digital legacy, put your care preferences into your own words, and write the letters that say what paperwork can't.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• What do I need in place? — Wills, powers, wishes
• Where everything is — A guide for your family
• Digital legacy — Accounts, photos, subscriptions
• Letter to my family — What paperwork can't say
• If I can't speak for myself — Care preferences, in your words

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
