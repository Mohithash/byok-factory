# Club Bulletin

**Category:** Social  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.clubbulletin`  
**Short description (≤80):** Newsletters and notices your members actually read

## Full description
Club Bulletin writes the communications for any community group: monthly newsletters from scrappy notes, event announcements in three lengths, welcome packs for new members, member spotlights and a critique of the poster from a photo.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Monthly newsletter — Scrappy notes into a clean issue
• Announce an event — One event, three lengths
• New member pack — Everything a newcomer needs to know
• Member spotlight — Questions or a finished piece
• Poster check — Photo → what to fix

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
