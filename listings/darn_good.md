# Darn Good

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.darngood`  
**Short description (≤80):** Mend clothes, shoes and bags so they last years

## Full description
Darn Good turns a hole, tear or broken zip into a repair plan from a photo, teaches darning, patching and sashiko stitch by stitch, sorts out shoes and bags, and builds a mending kit for what you actually own.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Fix this — Photo of the damage → plan
• Learn a stitch — Darning, sashiko, whipstitch, blanket stitch
• Zips, buttons, hems, elastic — The fiddly bits
• Shoes & bags — Resole, re-glue, condition, restitch
• Build my mending kit — Just the tools your wardrobe needs
