# Grocery Gauge

**Category:** Food & Drink  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.grocerygauge`  
**Short description (≤80):** Know the cost per serving before you cook it

## Full description
Grocery Gauge costs any recipe per serving, finds cheaper swaps that keep the dish the same, reads your receipt to show where the money went, and builds a weekly shop to a fixed number.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Cost a recipe — Per serving, honestly
• Cheaper swap — Same dish, lower bill
• Scan a receipt — Where did the money go?
• Shop to a number — A week of meals inside the budget
• Worth buying big? — Bulk, multibuy and freezer maths

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
