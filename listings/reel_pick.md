# Reel Pick

**Category:** Entertainment  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.reelpick`  
**Short description (≤80):** Movie nights, watch orders and spoiler-safe catch-ups

## Full description
Reel Pick picks films and shows the whole room will agree on, plans franchise watch orders, catches you up before a new season without spoiling it, sparks post-credits discussion and schedules marathons.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Pick tonight's film — For everyone in the room
• Watch order — Franchise or universe
• Catch me up — Spoiler-safe primer
• Post-film discussion — Questions and readings
• Marathon planner — A day or weekend of viewing

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
