# Coop Keeper

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.coopkeeper`  
**Short description (≤80):** Healthy hens, safe coops, more eggs

## Full description
Coop Keeper helps backyard chicken keepers triage flock health, predator-proof the coop from a photo, work out why the eggs stopped, feed by age, and integrate new birds without the bloodshed.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Sick hen check — Symptoms → likely causes and next steps
• Coop & predator audit — Photo → weak spots and fixes
• Why no eggs? — Egg-drop detective
• Feed & treats — Ration by age and purpose
• Add new birds — Integration without the bloodshed

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
