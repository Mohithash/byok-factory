## Full description
One Pan Wonder builds sheet-pan, skillet and one-pot dinners from what you have, staggers add-in times so nothing is raw or burnt, and keeps a weekly rotation with a single pan to wash.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Sheet-pan tonight — Protein + veg, roasted right
• Skillet formula — Stir-fry or sauté from scratch
• One-pot pasta or grain — Everything in one pot, no draining
• Weekly rotation — 5 one-pan dinners, one shop
• Rescue the pan — Soggy, burnt or unevenly cooked
