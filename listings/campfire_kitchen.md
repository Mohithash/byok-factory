# Campfire Kitchen

**Category:** Food & Drink  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.campfirekitchen`  
**Short description (≤80):** Camp meals planned, packed and cooked over fire

## Full description
Campfire Kitchen plans meals for a whole trip around your stove and cooler, packs the cooler so food stays safe for days, and turns out foil packets, Dutch-oven bakes and no-cook lunches at the pitch.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Plan the trip — Every meal, one packing list
• Pack the cooler — Keep it cold and safe
• Foil packet dinner — Build it, seal it, bury it in embers
• No-fire meals — Wet day, fire ban or just lazy
• Dutch-oven bake — Bread, cobbler or a stew for the whole pitch
