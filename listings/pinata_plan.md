## Full description
Piñata Plan runs your child's birthday party: a run sheet that keeps the energy right, games by age and space, allergy-safe food, invites, and a rescue plan when it rains.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Plan the party — Run sheet, prep list and budget
• Games by age — Fill the minutes without chaos
• Party food & cake — Quantities, allergies, make-ahead
• Invites & thank-yous — Wording for every message
• Rescue me — Rain, no-shows, a broken bouncy castle
