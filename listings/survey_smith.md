# Survey Smith

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.surveysmith`  
**Short description (≤80):** Surveys people finish and answers you can trust

## Full description
Survey Smith writes questionnaires from your research goal, rewrites leading or confusing questions, sizes your sample, themes open-ended answers and drafts invitations that lift response rates.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Write my survey — Goal → full questionnaire
• Fix my questions — Paste questions → bias removed
• How many responses? — Margin of error made simple
• Theme open answers — Paste responses → themes and quotes
• Invitation & reminders — Messages that lift response rate

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
