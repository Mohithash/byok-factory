# Silk & Set

**Category:** Food & Drink  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.silkandset`  
**Short description (≤80):** Custards, mousses and cheesecakes that set perfectly

## Full description
Silk & Set formulates custards, mousses, panna cotta and cheesecake with the right gelatin, egg and cream ratios, walks you through tempering and water baths, and diagnoses splits, cracks and weeping from a photo.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Set calculator — Gelatin or eggs for the texture you want
• Cheesecake planner — Baked, no-bake or Basque, sized to your tin
• Custard walkthrough — Temper eggs without scrambling
• Split, cracked or weeping? — Photo → cause and rescue
• Plate it — Turn a set dessert into a plated course

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
