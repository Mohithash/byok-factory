# Baby Bash

**Category:** Events  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.babybash`  
**Short description (≤80):** Host a shower the mum-to-be actually enjoys

## Full description
Baby Bash helps the friend or sister hosting a baby shower, sprinkle or sip-and-see: run sheet and budget, games nobody dreads, a pregnancy-safe menu, invite wording, and a gift log with thank-yous.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Plan the shower — Run sheet, checklist, budget
• Games people don't dread — Activities matched to the crowd
• Menu she can eat — Pregnancy-safe food and mocktails
• Invites & registry wording — Say it warmly, not grabbily
• Gift log & thank-yous — Track who gave what, write the notes

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
