# Offer Close

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.offerclose`  
**Short description (≤80):** Land the candidate you want, let the rest down well

## Full description
Offer Close handles the last mile of hiring for managers: selling the role to the candidate you want, running reference calls that reveal something, making the offer call, handling counter-offers and saying no to everyone else with grace.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Sell the role — A pitch built on what they care about
• Reference call — Twenty minutes that actually tell you something
• The offer call — Say the number and hold the room
• Counter-offer & holdouts — When they stall or get countered
• Decline with dignity — Rejections that keep doors open

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
