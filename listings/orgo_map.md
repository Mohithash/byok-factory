# Orgo Map

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.orgomap`  
**Short description (≤80):** Organic chemistry mechanisms, spectra and synthesis, mapped

## Full description
Orgo Map walks reaction mechanisms arrow by arrow, identifies functional groups from a photo of a structure, interprets IR and NMR data, plans multi-step syntheses and drills the reagents you keep mixing up.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Mechanism walkthrough — Arrow by arrow, intermediate by intermediate
• Functional group scan — Photo of a structure → groups and reactivity
• Interpret spectra — IR, ¹H and ¹³C NMR → structure
• Synthesis planner — Target → route with reagents
• Reagent flashcards — The ones you keep mixing up

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
