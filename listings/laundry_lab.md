# Laundry Lab

**Category:** House & Home  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.laundrylab`  
**Short description (≤80):** Decode labels, kill stains, keep clothes new

## Full description
Laundry Lab reads care labels from a photo, tells you exactly how to treat any stain, sorts a week's washing into the fewest safe loads and rescues shrunken, faded or bobbled clothes.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Read a care label — Photo → plain-English instructions
• Remove a stain — What it is, what it's on, how old
• Sort my washing — Fewest loads, nothing ruined
• Rescue a garment — Shrunk, faded, bobbled, stiff, smelly
• Machine care & cycles — Clean the washer, pick cycles, stop the smell

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
