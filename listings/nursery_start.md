# Nursery Start

**Category:** Parenting  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.nurserystart`  
**Short description (≤80):** Settle your baby or toddler into childcare, tear-free-ish

## Full description
Nursery Start helps you choose between nursery, childminder and nanny, plans the settling-in weeks, scripts the goodbye at drop-off, writes the key-worker handover sheet and decodes the illness policy and contract from a photo.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Nursery, childminder or nanny? — Compare for your hours and budget
• Settling-in plan — Gradual sessions that build trust
• Drop-off script — Short, warm, consistent
• All-about-me sheet — What the key worker needs to know
• Decode the policy — Photo of the contract or illness rules

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
