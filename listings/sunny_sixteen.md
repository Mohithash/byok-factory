# Sunny Sixteen

**Category:** Photography  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.sunnysixteen`  
**Short description (≤80):** Film photography: expose, develop and fix, frame by frame

## Full description
Sunny Sixteen is a film photographer's pocket assistant: exposure without a light meter, film stock picks, push/pull and reciprocity maths, home developing recipes, and a diagnosis of any negative or scan from a photo.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Meter this scene — Aperture and shutter with no meter
• Pick a film — Three stocks for the shoot and the look
• Push, pull & reciprocity — The maths and what to tell the lab
• Developing recipe — Times, dilutions, agitation
• Diagnose a negative — Photo of a negative, scan or print → cause
