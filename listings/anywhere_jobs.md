# Anywhere Jobs

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.anywherejobs`  
**Short description (≤80):** Land a real remote job, not a fake one

## Full description
Anywhere Jobs helps you find and win genuinely remote roles: it screens listings for scams and 'remote-ish' traps, rewrites your application around async skills, preps you for camera interviews and take-home tasks, and decodes location-based pay.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Is this listing legit? — Paste a job post, get a verdict
• Remote-proof my application — Show you can work async
• Camera interview prep — Video calls and async interviews
• Plan the take-home — Scope, time-box, deliver
• Decode location pay — Geo bands, currency, contractor vs employee

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
