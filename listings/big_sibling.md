# Big Sibling

**Category:** Parenting  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.bigsibling`  
**Short description (≤80):** Get your toddler ready for the new baby

## Full description
Big Sibling prepares a toddler for a new baby with a week-by-week timeline, plans the birth days and the first meeting, handles jealousy and regression, and protects one-on-one time once the baby is home.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Prep timeline — What to do each week before the due date
• Birth-day plan — Who has the toddler, what they hear
• First meeting — Introducing the baby
• Jealousy & regression — Hitting the baby, wanting a bottle
• Special time rituals — 10 minutes a day that fixes most things
