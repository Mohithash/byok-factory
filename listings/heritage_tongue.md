# Heritage Tongue

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.heritagetongue`  
**Short description (≤80):** Reconnect with the language your family speaks

## Full description
Heritage Tongue helps heritage speakers who understand but can't quite speak: family phrases, decoding relatives' voice notes and texts, festival greetings, and a gentle plan to talk with grandparents.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Phrases for a family moment — Say the right thing at the table
• Decode a message — Voice note, text, romanised chat
• Fix my sentence — Say it the grown-up way
• Festival greetings — The right words for the day
• 30-day speaking plan — From understanding to talking

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
