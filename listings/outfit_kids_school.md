Uniform & Co plans back-to-school lists, weekly family logistics, homework routines and communications with teachers.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Back-to-school list — Everything, by child
• Weekly logistics — Who, where, when
• Homework routine — Less nagging

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
