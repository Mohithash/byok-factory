# Latch Lab

**Category:** Parenting  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.latchlab`  
**Short description (≤80):** Breastfeeding and pumping, without the guesswork

## Full description
Latch Lab troubleshoots latch pain and supply worries, checks whether baby is getting enough, builds pumping schedules for going back to work, checks medications and handles blocked ducts and mastitis.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Fix the latch — Pain, clicking, shallow latch
• Is baby getting enough? — Nappies, weight, feeding pattern
• Pumping schedule — Back to work, building a stash
• Can I take this? — Medication & breastfeeding
• Blocked duct or mastitis? — Lumps, redness, fever

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
