# Insight Memo

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.insightmemo`  
**Short description (≤80):** Turn analysis into a memo people act on

## Full description
Insight Memo turns your findings into a one-page memo, a readout deck outline and plain-English number comparisons, and rehearses the questions stakeholders will fire back.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Write the memo — Findings → one page
• Readout deck outline — Slide by slide
• Make numbers land — Comparisons people remember
• Stakeholder questions — What they'll ask and how to answer
• Chart caption & takeaway — One chart, one sentence

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
