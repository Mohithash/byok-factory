# Tinsel Ops

**Category:** House & Home  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.tinselops`  
**Short description (≤80):** Christmas hosting with a timetable, not a meltdown

## Full description
Tinsel Ops runs Christmas at your house: an oven timetable that works backwards from serving time, beds and seating for everyone staying, a fair Secret Santa draw, Christmas Eve and morning plans for the kids, and a Boxing Day leftovers plan.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Oven timetable — Backwards from serving time
• Beds, seats & house prep — Who sleeps where, who sits where
• Secret Santa draw — Fair pairings and the announcement
• Christmas Eve & morning — The magic, scheduled
• Leftovers & Boxing Day — Use it all up safely

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
