# Tax Tidy

**Category:** Finance  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.taxtidy`  
**Short description (≤80):** Get organised for tax season

## Full description
Tax Tidy builds document checklists for your situation, explains common deductions in general terms, and turns receipts into categorised expense logs.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• What do I need? — Documents for filing
• Log a receipt — Photo → expense entry
• Can I deduct this? — General guidance
• Quarterly summary — Paste transactions

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
