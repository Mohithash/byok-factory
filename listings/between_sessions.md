# Between Sessions

**Category:** Health & Fitness  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.betweensessions`  
**Short description (≤80):** Get more from therapy in the days between

## Full description
Between Sessions helps you prepare for and debrief therapy, build the exposure ladders and activity schedules your therapist assigned, and decode any worksheet you've been handed.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Prep my session — Don't waste the first ten minutes
• Debrief a session — Capture it before it fades
• Exposure ladder — Fear → ten rungs you can climb
• Activity schedule — Behavioural activation for a flat week
• Decode a worksheet — Photo → what it wants from you

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
