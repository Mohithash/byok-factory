# Demo Director

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.demodirector`  
**Short description (≤80):** Product demos that tell a story, not a feature list

## Full description
Demo Director scripts persona-tailored product demos with a narrative arc, preps you for live questions and curveballs, critiques your script and writes the follow-up that keeps momentum.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Script a demo — Persona → narrative demo
• Q&A prep — Questions they'll ask live
• Critique my script — Paste it, get a director's notes
• Curveball rescue — Demo broke / went off-script
• Post-demo follow-up — Keep the momentum

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
