# Ferret Den

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.ferretden`  
**Short description (≤80):** Ferret-proof, feed and train your business of ferrets

## Full description
Ferret Den audits rooms for escape routes and swallow-hazards from a photo, plans meat-based diets, nip- and litter-trains kits and rescues, designs dig-box enrichment, and flags adrenal and insulinoma signs before they get serious.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Ferret-proof a room — Photo → gaps, hazards, escape routes
• Diet plan — Kibble check or raw menu
• Nip & litter training — Kits, rescues and biters
• Enrichment & dig boxes — Tire them out in 20 minutes
• Health red flags — Adrenal, insulinoma, blockages, ECE
