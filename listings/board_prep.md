# Board Prep

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.boardprep`  
**Short description (≤80):** Board meetings that end with decisions, not dread

## Full description
Board Prep builds your board deck outline and pre-read memo, drafts a timed agenda, anticipates the hard questions and turns your raw notes into clean minutes and action lists.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Board deck outline — Sections, slides, timings
• Pre-read memo — Facts → the memo they read beforehand
• Agenda & timing — Discussion time, not just reporting
• Hard questions — What they'll ask where you're weak
• Minutes from notes — Raw notes → formal minutes + actions

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
