# OKR Studio

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.okrstudio`  
**Short description (≤80):** Quarterly goals your team can actually measure

## Full description
OKR Studio drafts objectives and key results from your strategy, scores them for quality, cascades them to teams, runs weekly check-ins and grades the quarter honestly.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Draft company OKRs — Strategy → measurable objectives
• Score my OKRs — Outputs vs outcomes, measurability
• Cascade to a team — Team KRs that ladder up
• Weekly check-in — Confidence, blockers, next actions
• Grade the quarter — Honest scores and lessons

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
