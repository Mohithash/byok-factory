# Deck Draft

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.deckdraft`  
**Short description (≤80):** A pitch deck investors read to the last slide

## Full description
Deck Draft turns your startup's story into a slide-by-slide pitch deck, critiques the slides you already have from a photo, sizes your market bottom-up and drills you on the questions investors actually ask.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Build my deck — Slide-by-slide outline with headlines
• Critique a slide — Photo of a slide → fixes and a better headline
• Size the market — Bottom-up TAM / SAM / SOM
• Investor Q&A drill — The questions that follow the pitch
• One-pager & blurb — Teaser email, memo or 60-second script

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
