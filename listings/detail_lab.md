# Gloss Lab

**Category:** Auto & Vehicles  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.detaillab`  
**Short description (≤80):** Showroom shine from your own driveway

## Full description
Gloss Lab reads your paint from a photo, writes a safe wash and decontamination routine for your kit and time, plans paint correction and coatings, rescues interiors and prices detailing jobs if you do it for others.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Read my paint — Photo → what it needs
• Wash routine — Safe wash for your kit and time
• Correction & coating plan — Swirls → gloss → protection
• Interior rescue — Stains, smells, leather, screens
• Quote a detailing job — Price it for a customer

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
