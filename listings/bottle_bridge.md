# Bottle Bridge

**Category:** Parenting  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.bottlebridge`  
**Short description (≤80):** Bottles, formula and combi-feeding, done right

## Full description
Bottle Bridge fixes bottle refusal, teaches paced bottle feeding to any carer, plans combination feeding without losing supply, decodes a formula tin from a photo and troubleshoots fussy, windy or gulping bottle feeds.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Bottle refusal — Baby won't take the bottle
• Paced bottle feeding — Slow, responsive bottles
• Combination feeding plan — Swap feeds for bottles, keep supply
• Decode a formula tin — Photo → prep, amounts, safety
• Fussy bottle feeds — Gulping, wind, spit-up, tiny feeds
