# Conceive Kit

**Category:** Health & Fitness  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.conceivekit`  
**Short description (≤80):** Trying for a baby, with a plan and less panic

## Full description
Conceive Kit turns trying to conceive into a calm plan: preconception checklists for both of you, timing for this cycle, an honest two-week-wait companion, sperm-health basics and when-to-get-help guidance.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Preconception checklist — 3 months out, for both
• This cycle's timing — When to try, without a spreadsheet
• Two-week wait — Symptom-spotting, honestly
• When to get help — Timelines, tests, first appointment
• Sperm health basics — For the partner or donor conversation

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
