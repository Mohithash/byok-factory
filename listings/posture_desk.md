# Desk Doctor

**Category:** Health & Fitness  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.posturedesk`  
**Short description (≤80):** Fix the aches from sitting all day

## Full description
Desk Doctor sets up your workstation from a photo, prescribes micro-break routines, and builds mobility plans for neck, back, wrists and hips.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Check my desk — Photo → adjustments
• Micro-break routine — 2 minutes every hour
• Mobility plan — 10 min/day, 2 weeks
• Pain right now — What to do today

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.

## Tools
- 📷 **Check my desk** — Photo → adjustments
- ⏱️ **Micro-break routine** — 2 minutes every hour
- 🧘 **Mobility plan** — 10 min/day, 2 weeks
- 🩹 **Pain right now** — What to do today
