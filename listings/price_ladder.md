# Price Ladder

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.priceladder`  
**Short description (≤80):** Raise prices, add tiers, keep customers

## Full description
Price Ladder builds good-better-best tiers, proves what a discount really costs, scripts price increases and reads competitor pricing from a screenshot.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Build tiers — Good, better, best around your core offer
• What a discount costs — Extra units needed to break even
• Announce a price rise — The letter, the footer, the phone line
• Read competitor pricing — Screenshot → their tiers vs yours
• Price a custom job — Floor, target and stretch for bespoke work

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
