# Jewel Box

**Category:** Lifestyle

## Full description
Jewel Box styles jewellery and accessories to any outfit, matches necklaces to necklines, teaches metal mixing and stacking, reads hallmarks from a photo and shows how to clean and store every material.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Finish this outfit — Describe or photograph the outfit
• Necklace for this neckline — Length and style by neckline
• Stack & mix metals — Rings, bracelets, ear stacks
• Read a hallmark — Photo → metal, purity, era
• Clean & store — By material
