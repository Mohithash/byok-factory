# Calm Storm

**Category:** Parenting  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.calmstorm`  
**Short description (≤80):** Toddler tantrums: what to say, right now

## Full description
Calm Storm gives you the words in the middle of a toddler meltdown, finds triggers in your tantrum diary, plans the transitions that always blow up, repairs the relationship afterwards and tackles hitting and biting.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Right now — What to say in the next 60 seconds
• Trigger detective — Paste a week of tantrums
• Transition plan — Leaving the park without a fight
• Repair after the storm — Reconnect once everyone's calm
• Hitting & biting plan — Firm limits, no shame

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
