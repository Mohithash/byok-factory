# Focus Friend

**Category:** Health & Fitness  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.adhddaily`  
**Short description (≤80):** ADHD-friendly structure for real days

## Full description
Focus Friend builds low-friction daily plans, breaks tasks down to the first step, sets up body-doubling and dopamine-aware routines, and helps with transitions.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Plan today — Loose, forgiving
• Break it down — Until it's obvious
• Reset — Overwhelmed or frozen
• Morning routine — Launch sequence

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
