# Plain Speak

**Category:** Tools  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.plainspeak`  
**Short description (≤80):** Turn jargon into words anyone understands

## Full description
Plain Speak rewrites dense letters, policies, forms and instructions into plain language at the reading level you choose, and shows exactly what changed and why.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Rewrite in plain language — Same meaning, half the effort to read
• Readability report — Where readers get stuck and why
• Decode a letter or form — Photo → what it says and what to do
• Jargon glossary — Every technical term, defined plainly
• Explain it for… — The same idea for a specific reader

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
