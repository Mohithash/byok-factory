# Stage Ready

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.publicspeaker`  
**Short description (≤80):** Speeches, pitches and presentations that land

## Full description
Stage Ready structures talks, writes openings and closings, builds slide outlines, and rehearses you with tough questions.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Structure my talk — Message → outline
• Slide outline — Titles that make the point
• Rehearse Q&A — Tough questions
• Calm my nerves — Before you go on

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.

## Tools
- 🏗️ **Structure my talk** — Message → outline
- 🖥️ **Slide outline** — Titles that make the point
- ❓ **Rehearse Q&A** — Tough questions
- 🫁 **Calm my nerves** — Before you go on
