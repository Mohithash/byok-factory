# Objection Dojo

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.objectiondojo`  
**Short description (≤80):** Turn every 'no' into a next step

## Full description
Objection Dojo gives you calm, specific responses to price, timing, competitor and 'send me some info' objections, then drills you with role-play until they're second nature.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Handle this objection — Verbatim → 3 ways to respond
• Price pushback — Defend value without discounting
• Competitor comparison — When they name a rival
• Role-play drill — 5 curveballs with model answers
• My objection library — Prep before a new campaign

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
