# Meter Mate

**Category:** Books & Reference  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.metermate`  
**Short description (≤80):** Scansion, form and rhyme help for poets

## Full description
Meter Mate scans your lines for stress and syllables, checks a poem against its form, workshops it like a good reading group, and finds rhymes that fit the mood.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Scan my meter — Stress and syllable map per line
• Form check — Does it obey the form?
• Workshop this poem — Notes like a good reading group
• Rhyme & sound finder — Perfect, slant and vowel rhymes
• Today's prompt — Three prompts for your forms

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
