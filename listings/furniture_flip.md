# Furniture Flip

**Category:** House & Home  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.furnitureflip`  
**Short description (≤80):** Turn second-hand finds into pieces you love or sell

## Full description
Furniture Flip appraises second-hand furniture from a photo, plans refinishing step by step with the right products, fixes wobbles and water rings, and prices finished pieces to sell.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Should I buy it? — Photo → what it is, what it's worth
• Refinish plan — Sand, prime, paint or stain, step by step
• Fix a problem — Wobbles, sticky drawers, water rings, chips
• Handles, legs & details — Upgrades that change the whole piece
• Price & list it — What to charge and how to write the listing

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
