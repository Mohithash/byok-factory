# Charge Curve

**Category:** Auto & Vehicles  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.evcharge`  
**Short description (≤80):** Own an EV without range or bill anxiety

## Full description
Charge Curve plans road-trip charging stops around your car's real range, works out what home charging costs on your tariff, estimates winter range, teaches battery-health habits and decodes charger errors from a photo.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Plan charging stops — Route → stops, minutes, cost
• Home charging cost — Tariff → cost per month
• Winter range — How far today, really
• Battery health habits — Keep the pack young
• Decode a charger error — Photo of the screen or dash

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
