# Invisible Load

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.invisibleload`  
**Short description (≤80):** Share the mental load, not just the chores

## Full description
Invisible Load makes the hidden work of running a home visible — the noticing, planning and remembering — then helps couples split it fairly, talk about it without a fight, and keep the deal alive.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Inventory the load — Everything that keeps this house running
• Re-split fairly — Full ownership, start to finish
• Raise it with my partner — Without 'you never…'
• Weekly household meeting — 15 minutes, one agenda
• The deal slipped — Back to doing it all again

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
