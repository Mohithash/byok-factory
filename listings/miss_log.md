# Miss Log

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.misslog`  
**Short description (≤80):** Your wrong answers, analysed into a score plan

## Full description
Miss Log turns the questions you get wrong on any standardised test into an error taxonomy, a weakness heat-map, targeted re-drills and a score projection, so every practice test changes what you study next.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Analyse a practice test — Misses → causes → the one fix
• Weakness heat-map — Topics ranked by miss rate and trend
• Targeted re-drill — Questions engineered against your usual cause
• Project my score — Where the trend lands on test day
• Weekly review — Five questions, one table, next week's plan

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
