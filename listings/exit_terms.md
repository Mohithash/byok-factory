# Exit Terms

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.exitterms`  
**Short description (≤80):** Redundancy, settlements and leaving on the right terms

## Full description
Exit Terms helps you leave a job well: understand a redundancy or settlement offer, check the maths, negotiate references and notice, decode non-compete and confidentiality clauses, and write a resignation that keeps doors open.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Decode my offer — Redundancy or settlement, line by line
• Exit maths — What you should walk away with
• Negotiate the package — Ask for more, gracefully
• Non-compete and confidentiality — What you can do after you leave
• Resignation letter — Short, gracious, correct dates

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
