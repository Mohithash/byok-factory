# Bin Zero

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.binzero`  
**Short description (≤80):** Shrink your bin, one swap at a time

## Full description
Bin Zero audits what you throw away, finds the swaps that actually pay off, tells you how to dispose of the awkward stuff and plans package-free shopping trips that fit your week.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Audit my bin — Photo of a day's rubbish → what to divert
• Is this swap worth it? — Cost, payback and whether it lasts
• What do I do with this? — Reuse, recycle or safe disposal
• Package-free shop — Plan a refill or bulk run
• Zero-waste room reset — Kitchen, bathroom, cleaning or laundry

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
