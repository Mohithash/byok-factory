# Warm Enough

**Category:** House & Home  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.warmenough`  
**Short description (≤80):** Heat the home you have for less, without the damp

## Full description
Warm Enough builds a heating schedule for your routine, finds draughts from a photo, balances and bleeds radiators, compares the real cost of heaters and keeps damp and mould away when you heat less.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Heating schedule — Thermostat times for your real week
• Draught detective — Photo of a window, door or floor → fixes
• Radiators & controls — Bleed, balance, TRVs, boiler flow
• Heater cost per hour — Which heater, and heat the person or the room?
• Damp & mould when heating less — Condensation, ventilation, humidity

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
