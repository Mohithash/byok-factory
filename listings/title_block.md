# Title Block

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.titleblock`  
**Short description (≤80):** Engineering jobs: portfolio, technical rounds, PE path

## Full description
Title Block helps mechanical, civil, electrical, chemical and industrial engineers land roles: portfolio entries with real outcomes, technical interview drills by discipline, EIT-to-PE licensure roadmaps, posting decoders and offer comparisons.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Portfolio entry — One project, told like an engineer
• Technical drill — Fundamentals questions for your discipline
• EIT to PE roadmap — Exams, experience, references
• Decode a posting — Required vs nice-to-have, plus gaps
• Compare offers — Base, bonus, OT exemption, PE support

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
