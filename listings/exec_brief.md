# Exec Brief

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.execbrief`  
**Short description (≤80):** Say it in five lines — leaders will read it

## Full description
Exec Brief turns long updates into bottom-line-first briefs, drafts bad news for the people above you, escalates blockers without sounding like a complaint and answers terse executive questions without a wall of text.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• BLUF it — Long update → five-line brief
• Deliver bad news up — Miss, slip or mistake — say it right
• Escalate properly — Blocked? Raise it without whining
• Answer an exec's question — Terse reply, no wall of text
• Reply to an angry exec — A heated message from above — answer it well

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
