# Profile Lift

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.linkedinlift`  
**Short description (≤80):** LinkedIn that gets recruiters to message you

## Full description
Profile Lift rewrites headlines and About sections, turns job history into achievement bullets, and drafts posts that build credibility.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Headline & About — Rewrite
• Achievement bullets — For one role
• Write a post — Credibility, not cringe
• Recruiter reply — Say yes, no, or maybe

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
