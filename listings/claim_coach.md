# Claim Coach

**Category:** Tools  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.claimcoach`  
**Short description (≤80):** Small claims court, prepared step by step

## Full description
Claim Coach helps you decide whether a small claim is worth it, write the letter before action, organise evidence into a bundle, draft the claim itself and rehearse the hearing.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Is it worth it? — Costs, odds, time — honestly
• Letter before action — The letter that often settles it
• Evidence bundle — Order your proof like a lawyer would
• Draft the claim — Particulars of claim, plain and complete
• Hearing day — What to say, what they'll ask

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
