# Reading Rocket

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.readingrocket`  
**Short description (≤80):** From sounding out to reading smoothly and loving it

## Full description
Reading Rocket builds fluency and confidence in 6–10-year-olds who can decode but read slowly or reluctantly: levelled passages with a words-per-minute target, reader's-theatre scripts, comprehension checks and book matches for kids who say reading is boring.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Levelled passage — A short text for repeated reading
• Reader's theatre — A script to perform together
• Book match — Books they'll actually finish
• Check comprehension — After they read: did it go in?
• Listen to them read — Paste what you noticed, get a plan
