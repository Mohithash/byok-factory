# Room Let

**Category:** House & Home  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.roomlet`  
**Short description (≤80):** Rent out a room in your home, calmly and fairly

## Full description
Room Let helps homeowners and tenants let a room to a lodger or flatmate: pricing it right, writing the ad, screening applicants, setting house rules and an agreement, and handling problems without falling out.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Price the room — Rent, deposit, bills
• Write the ad — A listing that attracts the right person
• Screen applicants — Who to shortlist and what to verify
• House rules & agreement — Clauses and friendly wording
• Handle a problem — Late rent, mess, guests, moving out

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
