# IVF Companion

**Category:** Medical  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.ivfcompanion`  
**Short description (≤80):** Your cycle, your protocol, one calm place

## Full description
IVF Companion explains each stage of fertility treatment, turns your clinic protocol into a day-by-day schedule, checks side effects, preps transfer day and decodes scan, embryo and hCG results.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Build my schedule — Paste the protocol
• Explain this stage — What's happening and why
• Side-effect check — Bloating, pain, headaches, mood
• Transfer day prep — What to do, what not to worry about
• Decode results — Scans, embryo grades, beta hCG

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
