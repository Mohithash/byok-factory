# Scoop Lab

**Category:** Food & Drink

## Full description
Scoop Lab balances ice cream, gelato and sorbet bases for your machine (or no machine), formulates flavours from sugar and fat targets, and fixes icy, grainy or rock-hard batches.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Balance a base — Fat, sugar, solids for your machine
• Formulate a flavour — Mix-ins, ripples and infusions
• No-machine methods — Freezer-and-fork, no-churn, pint spinner
• Fix a batch — Icy, grainy, gummy, rock-hard
• Sundae & party — Sauces, toppings, quantities for a crowd
