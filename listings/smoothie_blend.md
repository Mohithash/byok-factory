# Blend Book

**Category:** Food & Drink  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.smoothieblend`  
**Short description (≤80):** Smoothies built for your goal, blender and week

## Full description
Blend Book writes smoothies around your goal and the produce you have, preps a week of freezer packs, fixes texture and taste, and estimates the nutrition of what is in the cup.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Blend from what I have — Produce + goal → 3 recipes
• Freezer packs — A week of grab-and-blend bags
• Fix my smoothie — Too thick, bitter, separated…
• Estimate nutrition — What's really in the cup
• Scan my fruit bowl — Photo → blend it before it turns

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
