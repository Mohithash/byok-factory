# Ayah Companion

**Category:** Books & Reference  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.ayahstudy`  
**Short description (≤80):** Study, reflect on and memorise the Qur'an at your pace

## Full description
Ayah Companion helps you study surahs with tafsir summaries and key Arabic words, build a realistic hifz schedule with revision built in, plan Ramadan reading and find ayat that speak to your day.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Study a surah — Context, themes, tafsir highlights
• Hifz plan — Memorise with revision built in
• Tajweed check — Rules present in the ayat you recite
• Ayat on a theme — Find what the Qur'an says about…
• Ramadan reading plan — Finish the Qur'an, or study deeply

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
