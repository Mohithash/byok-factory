# Fourth Trimester

**Category:** Medical  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.fourthtri`  
**Short description (≤80):** Recovery for the parent, not just the baby

## Full description
Fourth Trimester guides your own recovery after birth: healing by birth type, bleeding and pain checks, mood check-ins, scripts to ask for help, and a plan for the six-week check.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Recovery this week — What's healing, what to do
• Is this normal? — Bleeding, pain, wound, breasts, bladder
• Mood check-in — Baby blues, anxiety, or more
• Ask for help — Scripts for partner, family, visitors, work
• Six-week check prep — Questions, contraception, exercise, sex

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
