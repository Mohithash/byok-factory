# Eco Cart

**Category:** Shopping  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.ecocart`  
**Short description (≤80):** Shop with a lighter footprint, no greenwash

## Full description
Eco Cart audits sustainability claims on any product from a photo, decodes certifications, tells you when buying second-hand beats buying green, finds lower-impact alternatives ranked by what actually matters, and builds buy-it-for-life shortlists.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Greenwash check — Photo of the packaging → claims audit
• Decode a certification — FSC, B Corp, GOTS, Fairtrade, Energy Star…
• Lower-impact alternative — Ranked by what matters to you
• Buy it for life — What lasts decades and what to look for
• New or second-hand? — When used is the greener buy

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
