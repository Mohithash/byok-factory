# Road Warrior

**Category:** Travel & Local  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.roadwarrior`  
**Short description (≤80):** Business trips that run like clockwork

## Full description
Road Warrior handles the frequent business traveller's trip: one-bag packing for meetings, day plans with real travel buffers and jet-lag timing, expense reports from receipt photos, client-dinner picks with cultural etiquette, and making the flight hours count.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• One-bag packing — Carry-on only, meeting-ready
• Trip agenda — Meetings, transfers, jet lag, buffers
• Expense a receipt — Photo → line item
• Client dinner — Where, what and how to behave
• Make the flight count — Offline work plan for the hours in the air

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
