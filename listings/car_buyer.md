# Car Sense

**Category:** Auto & Vehicles  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.carbuyer`  
**Short description (≤80):** Buy, sell and maintain a car without getting fleeced

## Full description
Car Sense compares models for your needs, prepares inspection checklists and haggling scripts, decodes listings and plans maintenance.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Shortlist models — For your needs and budget
• Inspect a used car — Checklist + questions
• Decode a listing — Photo or paste
• Maintenance plan — By km and age

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.

## Tools

- 🔍 **Inspect a used car** — Checklist + questions
- 📷 **Decode a listing** — Photo or paste
