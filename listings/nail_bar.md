# Nail Bar

**Category:** Beauty  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.nailbar`  
**Short description (≤80):** Salon-level nails at home, and healthier ones too

## Full description
Nail Bar designs nail sets for any occasion, walks you through a home manicure or gel application, compares gel, acrylic, dip and press-ons for your life, and spots nail health problems from a photo.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Design a set — Occasion + vibe → looks
• Home manicure — Prep, paint, cure, finish
• Gel, acrylic, dip or press-on? — The right system for your life
• Check my nails — Photo → what the nail is saying
• Remove safely — Gel, acrylic, dip or glue

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
