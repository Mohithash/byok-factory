# Hear Clear

**Category:** Medical  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.hearclear`  
**Short description (≤80):** Live well with hearing loss and hearing aids

## Full description
Hear Clear troubleshoots hearing aids, plans restaurants and gatherings so you can follow the conversation, gives you the words to tell people what you need, and sets up phones and TVs for captions and clarity.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Fix my hearing aid — Whistling, muffled, dead
• Plan a noisy occasion — Restaurant, party, church
• Tell people what I need — Words that don't feel awkward
• Phone, TV & doorbell — Captions and clarity settings
• Audiologist visit — Questions and a symptom summary

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
