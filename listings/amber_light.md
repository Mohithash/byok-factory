# Amber Light

**Category:** Auto & Vehicles  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.amberlight`  
**Short description (≤80):** Know what your car is telling you before the garage does

## Full description
Amber Light decodes dashboard warning lights from a photo, diagnoses noises and smells, sanity-checks a mechanic's quote line by line, walks you through a roadside breakdown and explains an inspection failure.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Decode a warning light — Photo → meaning, urgency
• Noise or smell — Describe it, get the suspects
• Check a garage quote — Paste it → fair or fleeced
• Broken down — Roadside steps right now
• Failed inspection — MOT or safety test explained

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
