# All Hands

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.allhands`  
**Short description (≤80):** Town halls people don't mute

## Full description
All Hands plans and runs your company or department town hall — the run-sheet, each segment's script, triage of the anonymous questions, the live answer to a spiky one and the follow-up that proves people were heard.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Build the run-sheet — Segments, timing, owners
• Script a segment — Numbers, a change or a win — said well
• Triage the questions — From the anonymous form to a Q&A plan
• Answer this live — A spiky question, 20 seconds to answer
• After the meeting — Recap, open questions, pulse check

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
