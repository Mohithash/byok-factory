# Rights Desk

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.rightsdesk`  
**Short description (≤80):** Know who owns the work before you deliver it

## Full description
Rights Desk helps designers, writers, developers and photographers understand and price usage rights, respond when a client wants source files or full ownership, check whether they can show work in a portfolio, and act when work is used without payment.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Price a licence — Usage, territory, duration → fee
• They want the source files — Respond to ownership and file requests
• Can I show this? — Portfolio, credits and NDAs
• Used without paying — Escalate unpaid or unlicensed use
• My rights terms — A rights section for your contract

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
