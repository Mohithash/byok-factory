# Defence Line

**Category:** Tools  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.defenceline`  
**Short description (≤80):** Been sent a claim or demand? Respond properly

## Full description
Defence Line helps people on the receiving end of a demand letter, debt collector or small claim: decode the document, meet the deadline, draft a response or defence, weigh a settlement and check for a counterclaim.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Decode the letter — Photo or paste → what it really means
• Reply to a demand — Firm, careful, no admissions
• Draft my defence — Answer a court claim point by point
• Settle or fight? — Numbers before feelings
• Counterclaim check — Do they owe you too?

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
