# Suit Up

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.suitup`  
**Short description (≤80):** Men's style rules, decoded for real life

## Full description
Suit Up decodes dress codes from any invite, checks fit from a mirror photo, builds a foundation menswear wardrobe on your budget and matches shoes, belts and watches so you always look put together.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Decode the dress code — Invite → exactly what to wear
• Fit check — Mirror photo → what to fix
• Foundation wardrobe — 12 pieces that cover 90% of life
• Shoes, belt, watch — Match leathers and metals
• Level up a casual look — Same pieces, sharper

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
