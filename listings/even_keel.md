# Even Keel

**Category:** Finance  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.evenkeel`  
**Short description (≤80):** Pay yourself a steady salary from lumpy freelance income

## Full description
Even Keel turns feast-and-famine freelance income into a steady monthly salary: a buffer target, tax and holiday pots, pre-funded time off, a lean-month cash plan and a monthly review that fits on one screen.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Pay myself a salary — From the last 6–12 months
• Set up my pots — Tax, holiday, sick, pension
• Take time off — Pre-fund a holiday or a break
• Lean-month mode — The pipeline dried up
• Monthly review — Numbers, late invoices, one decision

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
