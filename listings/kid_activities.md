# Rainy Day Box

**Category:** Parenting  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.kidactivities`  
**Short description (≤80):** Kids' activities from what's in the house

## Full description
Rainy Day Box invents age-appropriate activities from household items, plans birthday parties and screen-free afternoons, and turns chores into games.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Something to do now — From what you have
• Birthday party — Plan it
• Chores as games — Get help without nagging
• Free outing — Nearby, low cost

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
