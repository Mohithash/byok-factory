# Room Runner

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.roomrunner`  
**Short description (≤80):** Workshops that end with decisions, not sticky notes

## Full description
Room Runner designs workshops and working sessions — kickoffs, problem-solving, prioritisation, decisions — with timed agendas, verbatim facilitator scripts, the right decision method, purposeful openers and moves for dominant voices, silence and derailers.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Design a workshop — Outcome → timed agenda
• Pick a decision method — Vote, consent, advice, or one owner?
• Someone is derailing — Dominant voice, silence, tangents, the boss
• Facilitator script — Every instruction, word for word
• Opener & energy plan — Warm-ups with a purpose, no cringe

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
