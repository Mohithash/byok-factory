# Brew Day

**Category:** Food & Drink  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.homebrewbeer`  
**Short description (≤80):** Design, brew and troubleshoot beer at home

## Full description
Brew Day designs beer recipes for your kit and batch size, writes a brew-day timeline with clock times, calculates priming sugar and ABV, and diagnoses stuck ferments and off-flavours.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Design a recipe — Style + target ABV → grain, hops, yeast
• Brew-day timeline — Clock times from heat-up to pitch
• Fermentation check — Is it stuck? Is it done?
• Fix an off-flavour — Taste → cause → fix
• Bottling calculator — Priming sugar + carbonation

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
