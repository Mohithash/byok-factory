# Vendor Scout

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.vendorscout`  
**Short description (≤80):** Choose tools and suppliers with a scorecard, not a hunch

## Full description
Vendor Scout builds weighted evaluation scorecards, compares vendors from your notes and quotes, writes RFI questions, flags contract red flags and estimates the true total cost of ownership.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Build a scorecard — Weighted criteria and demo questions
• Compare vendors — Notes and quotes → a decision
• Write an RFI — Questions vendors must answer
• Contract red flags — Paste terms → what to push back on
• True cost — Quote + hidden costs → 3-year TCO

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
