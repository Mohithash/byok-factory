# Nomad Base

**Category:** Travel & Local  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.nomadbase`  
**Short description (≤80):** Pick a base, count your days, keep the routine

## Full description
Nomad Base helps remote workers choose their next base city, compare long-stay and nomad-visa options in general terms, count days for tax and visa limits, set up a productive month anywhere and pack for it.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Pick my next base — Cities scored for your work
• Long-stay options — Tourist, nomad visa, residence
• Count my days — Tax and visa limits at a glance
• Set up the month — Land Sunday, productive Monday
• Carry-on office — Everything in one bag

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
