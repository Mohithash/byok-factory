# Gather Round

**Category:** Social  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.gatherround`  
**Short description (≤80):** Start a meetup people keep coming back to

## Full description
Gather Round helps you launch and run a recurring meetup — a walking group, language exchange, sketching club, parents' coffee morning, anything — with a six-week launch plan, listings that fill the room, a first-timer welcome script and fixes for flaky RSVPs.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Launch plan — From idea to first six events
• Write the listing — Event copy for every channel
• First-timer welcome — The host's first 15 minutes
• Fix flaky RSVPs — Turn 20 yeses into 15 people
• Hand over & grow — Co-hosts, splits and stepping back

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
