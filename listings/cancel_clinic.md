# Cancel Clinic

**Category:** Tools  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.cancelclinic`  
**Short description (≤80):** Cancel subscriptions, gyms and contracts without the trap

## Full description
Cancel Clinic finds the exit from gym memberships, subscriptions, phone and broadband contracts and auto-renewals: cooling-off periods, notice rules, the exact cancellation message and the script for the retention call.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Can I cancel? — Paste the terms, get the exit
• Cancellation notice — Written, dated, undeniable
• Retention call script — Stay polite, leave anyway
• Charged after cancelling — Stop the payments and get them back
• Subscription audit — Paste a statement, find the leaks

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
