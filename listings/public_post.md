# Public Post

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.publicpost`  
**Short description (≤80):** Government jobs: applications that actually get referred

## Full description
Public Post helps you get hired into federal, state, local and civil-service roles: converting a private-sector résumé into the long government format, writing KSA and competency statements, decoding announcements, preparing for clearance and background checks, and scored panel interviews.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Government résumé — Convert your CV to the long format
• KSA / competency statement — Selection criteria and behaviour examples
• Decode the announcement — Grade, series, who may apply, questionnaire
• Clearance & background — Prepare the forms without the panic
• Structured interview — Scored panels, STAR every time

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
