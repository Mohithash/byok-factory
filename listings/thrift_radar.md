# Thrift Radar

**Category:** Shopping  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.thriftradar`  
**Short description (≤80):** Find the gems, skip the junk, flip the rest

## Full description
Thrift Radar helps you judge whether a second-hand find is worth the price, date and authenticate vintage from labels, plan efficient thrift runs, rework finds and write resale listings that sell.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Worth it? — Photo + price → verdict
• Date & authenticate — Label photo → era and red flags
• Plan a thrift run — Route, list and tactics
• Rework a find — Alter, crop, dye, upcycle
• Write a resale listing — Title, description, price

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
