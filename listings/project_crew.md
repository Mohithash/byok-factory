# Project Crew

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.projectcrew`  
**Short description (≤80):** Group projects without the group chat meltdown

## Full description
Project Crew sets up a team charter, splits the work fairly, turns messy meetings into minutes with owners, scripts the awkward conversation with the teammate who has gone quiet, and writes honest peer assessments.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Team charter — Roles, rules and a kickoff message
• Split the work — Brief → tasks, owners, dates
• Minutes from my notes — Rough notes → decisions and actions
• The awkward conversation — Someone's not pulling their weight
• Peer assessment — Fair, evidenced, not petty

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
