# Booch Lab

**Category:** Food & Drink  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.kombuchalab`  
**Short description (≤80):** Kombucha and jun, batch after batch

## Full description
Booch Lab schedules first and second ferments for your kitchen temperature, designs flavours that actually carbonate, checks SCOBY health from a photo, and troubleshoots vinegar, flat bottles and mould.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Plan a batch — Tea, sugar, starter and dates
• Second-ferment flavour — Fruit, herbs, fizz
• Check my SCOBY — Photo → healthy, yeasty or mould
• Troubleshoot — Vinegary, flat, slimy, fruit flies
• SCOBY hotel & pause — Holidays, breaks, sharing

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
