# Green Room

**Category:** Events  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.greenroom`  
**Short description (≤80):** Run an am-dram show from auditions to curtain call

## Full description
Green Room is the stage manager's brain for community theatre, school shows and youth productions: audition notices, rehearsal schedules that respect people's evenings, props and costume lists from the script, front-of-house rotas and break-even ticket prices, plus programme and publicity copy.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Audition pack — Notice, pieces and panel checklist
• Rehearsal schedule — Calls that respect people's lives
• Props & costume — Production lists from the script
• Front of house — Ticket prices, rota and house rules
• Programme & publicity — Director's note, posts and listing

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
