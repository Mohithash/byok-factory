# Manage Up

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.manageup`  
**Short description (≤80):** Get the most out of the boss you actually have

## Full description
Manage Up decodes your manager's style, preps you for the conversations that matter — more scope, a disagreement, a brand-new boss — and designs an update rhythm that stops them micromanaging or vanishing.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Decode my manager — How they think, decide and judge
• Make the ask — Scope, budget, headcount, backing
• Disagree with my boss — Push back without the fallout
• New boss playbook — First 30 days with a new manager
• Keep them informed — Enough updates to stop the check-ins

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
