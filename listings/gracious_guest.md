# Gracious Guest

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.graciousguest`  
**Short description (≤80):** Be the guest everyone is glad they invited

## Full description
Gracious Guest prepares you for a holiday or life-cycle ceremony in a tradition you didn't grow up in — a Diwali dinner, Eid, a Seder, a Sikh wedding, a Buddhist funeral, a quinceañera — with what to wear, bring, say and avoid.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Holiday guest guide — Invited to celebrate a festival
• Ceremony guest guide — Weddings, funerals, namings, coming-of-age
• Write a message — Congratulations or condolences done right
• Choose a gift — Right gift, right amount, right wrapping
• Explain it to my kids — So they behave and enjoy it

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.
