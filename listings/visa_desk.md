# Visa Desk

**Category:** Travel & Local  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.visadesk`  
**Short description (≤80):** Every document, deadline and appointment in one place

## Full description
Visa Desk turns confusing visa requirements into document checklists, back-planned timelines, appointment prep and plain-English explanations of official letters — for any country's visa or residence permit.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Document checklist — What to gather for your visa type
• Back-plan my timeline — From today to arrival
• Appointment prep — Biometrics, consulate, interview
• Decode a letter — Photo of an official letter → plain English
• Refusal or delay — What to do next

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
