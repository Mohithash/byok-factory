# Cue Point

**Category:** Music & Audio  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.cuepoint`  
**Short description (≤80):** DJ sets that flow, transitions that land

## Full description
Cue Point builds DJ sets with an energy arc, suggests transitions between two exact tracks, drills beatmatching and harmonic mixing on your gear, digs for tracks and preps you for the first gig.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Plan a set — Slots, BPMs, keys and an energy arc
• Mix A into B — Bar counts, EQ moves, alternatives
• Practice drill — Beatmatching, phrasing, EQ, keys
• Gig prep — Gear, USBs, soundcheck and the room
• Crate digging — 12 tracks that fit a sound and a slot

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
