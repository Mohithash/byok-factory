# Scope Guard

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.scopeguard`  
**Short description (≤80):** Stop scope creep before it eats your margin

## Full description
Scope Guard helps freelancers and agencies spot scope creep the moment it appears, price change orders, set up retainers, and put kill fees, revision limits and payment milestones into plain-English terms.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Is this scope creep? — Compare the ask with the deal
• Write a change order — Price and paper the extra work
• Design a retainer — Recurring work, clear rules
• Protective clauses — Kill fee, revisions, late payment
• Red-flag a brief — Screen an inquiry before you quote

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
