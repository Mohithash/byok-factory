# Allergy Safe

**Category:** Health & Fitness  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.allergysafe`  
**Short description (≤80):** Eat out and cook safely with food allergies

## Full description
Allergy Safe scans labels and menus for your allergens, writes chef cards in any language, and plans safe meals and travel.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Scan a label — Photo → verdict
• Scan a menu — Photo → safe picks
• Chef card — In any language
• Safe recipe — Swap out allergens

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
