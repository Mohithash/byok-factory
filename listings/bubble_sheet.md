# Bubble Sheet

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.bubblesheet`  
**Short description (≤80):** SAT and ACT prep that targets what you actually miss

## Full description
Bubble Sheet drills SAT and ACT sections with exam-format questions, explains every wrong answer, teaches the grammar and maths rules the tests reuse, and plans your timing for test day.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Section drill — Exam-format questions on one section
• Explain my misses — Paste wrong answers → the pattern behind them
• Grammar & maths rules — The rules as the test disguises them
• Solve from a photo — Snap a question, get the fastest route
• Test-day timing plan — Pacing, checkpoints and skip rules

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
