# Quote Van

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.quotevan`  
**Short description (≤80):** Job quotes tradespeople can send from the driveway

## Full description
Quote Van turns a job description or photo into a priced quote for plumbers, electricians, decorators and builders, and handles variations, deposits and payment terms.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Build a quote — Description → itemised, ready-to-send quote
• Estimate from a photo — Snap the job, get a range
• Price a variation — They changed their mind mid-job
• Deposits & terms — Stage payments and plain-English T&Cs
• Chase a quote — Turn a sent quote into a booked job

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
