# Body Calm

**Category:** Health & Fitness  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.bodycalm`  
**Short description (≤80):** Release stress where your body actually holds it

## Full description
Body Calm works on stress from the neck down: progressive muscle relaxation scripts, a tension map from how you feel, shake-it-off movement resets and a body scan for sleep.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Progressive relaxation — Tense-and-release, timed
• Tension map — Describe it → what to release first
• Shake it off — A movement reset in under 3 minutes
• Body scan for sleep — Head to toe, lights off
• Daily unclench cues — Tie tiny releases to things you already do

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
