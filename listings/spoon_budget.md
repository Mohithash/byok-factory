# Spoon Budget

**Category:** Medical  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.spoonbudget`  
**Short description (≤80):** Pacing for chronic fatigue, fibromyalgia and long COVID

## Full description
Spoon Budget helps people with ME/CFS, fibromyalgia, long COVID or other energy-limiting conditions plan days within their energy envelope, spot boom-and-bust cycles, get through crashes and explain their limits to others.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Plan today — Fit tasks to your spoons
• Boom-bust check — Paste a couple of weeks
• Crash plan — Get through the next 72 hours
• Explain my limits — Scripts for work, family, friends
• Gentle movement check — Only if you're stable

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
