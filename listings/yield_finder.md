# Yield Finder

**Category:** Finance  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.yieldfinder`  
**Short description (≤80):** Know if a rental property pays before you buy it

## Full description
Yield Finder analyses a rental property purchase on gross and net yield, monthly cash flow and stress-tested rates, compares two deals side by side and lays out a strategy from refurb to exit.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Analyse a deal — Yield, cash flow, cash needed
• Compare two deals — Side by side on what matters
• Stress test — Rates up, rent down, voids doubled
• Refurb to exit — Phases, costs, exit options
• Research the area — What to verify before offering

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
