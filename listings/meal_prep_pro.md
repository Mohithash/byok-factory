# Meal Prep Pro

**Category:** Food & Drink  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.mealpreppro`  
**Short description (≤80):** A week of lunches in one Sunday session

## Full description
Meal Prep Pro plans batch-cooking sessions that fit your macros, containers and budget, with a shopping list and a cook order that finishes everything at once.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Plan my week — Lunches + dinners from one session
• Hit my macros — Recipes for a protein/carb/fat target
• Cook from my fridge — Photo → what to prep
• Storage & reheating — Keep prepped food safe and tasty

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
