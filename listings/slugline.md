# Slugline

**Category:** Art & Design  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.slugline`  
**Short description (≤80):** Screenplay format and scene notes in seconds

## Full description
Slugline converts rough scenes into correctly formatted screenplay pages, gives reader-style coverage on each scene, writes loglines, and checks your outline against a beat sheet.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Format this scene — Rough prose → screenplay page
• Scene notes — Coverage on one scene
• Logline & synopsis — Pitch it in one sentence, then 150 words
• Subtext pass — Cut the on-the-nose lines
• Beat sheet check — Outline vs 15 beats with page targets

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
