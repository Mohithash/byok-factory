# Shloka Sense

**Category:** Books & Reference  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.shlokasense`  
**Short description (≤80):** The Gita and Upanishads, one verse at a time

## Full description
Shloka Sense explains verses from the Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads and other Hindu texts with Sanskrit key terms, classical commentaries and a practical reflection, and helps you chant, memorise and apply them.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Explain a verse — Sanskrit, meaning, commentaries
• Chapter overview — Where you are in the Gita's arc
• Bring a dilemma — What would the text say to this?
• Learn to chant — Meter, pauses, pronunciation
• Verse for today — A short morning reflection
