# Coach Mode

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.coachmode`  
**Short description (≤80):** Ask, don't tell: coaching skills for managers

## Full description
Coach Mode helps managers develop people instead of solving their problems for them — coaching questions for the problem someone just brought you, GROW session plans, learning debriefs after a mistake, and an honest critique of your own coaching.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• They brought me a problem — Coach it instead of solving it
• GROW session plan — Goal, Reality, Options, Will — in 30 minutes
• They didn't do it — A commitment slipped — hold the line, keep the trust
• Critique my coaching — Paste what you said in a 1:1
• They messed up — A learning debrief, not a telling-off

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
