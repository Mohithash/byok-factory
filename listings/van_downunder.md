# Van Down Under

**Category:** Travel & Local

## Full description
Van Down Under plans campervan holidays in Australia and New Zealand: routes with honest driving days, the right van for your crew, where you can legally camp tonight, outback and gravel-road safety, and a daily cost sheet for fuel, sites and food.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Plan the route — Region loop with driving days
• Pick the van — Berths, self-contained, budget
• Camp legally tonight — Free, cheap or holiday park
• Outback & gravel safety — Water, fuel, road trains, wildlife
• Daily cost sheet — Fuel, sites, food, ferries

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
