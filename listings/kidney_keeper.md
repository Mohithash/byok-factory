# Kidney Keeper

**Category:** Medical  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.kidneykeeper`  
**Short description (≤80):** Eat and live well with chronic kidney disease

## Full description
Kidney Keeper helps people with CKD read their eGFR and blood results, plan meals that respect potassium, phosphorus, sodium and protein limits, check labels from a photo and manage fluids on hot days and sick days.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Explain my results — eGFR, creatinine, potassium, ACR
• Plan kidney-safe meals — 3 days within your limits
• Label check — Photo → hidden phosphates & salt
• Fluid & sick-day plan — Hot days, travel, illness
• Food swap finder — A kidney-friendlier version

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
