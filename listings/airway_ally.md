# Airway Ally

**Category:** Medical  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.airwayally`  
**Short description (≤80):** Asthma control, inhaler technique and trigger tracking

## Full description
Airway Ally helps people with asthma draft a written action plan to review with their clinician, perfect inhaler technique, find triggers in a symptom diary and prepare for exercise, travel and pollen season.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Draft my action plan — Green / amber / red zones
• Inhaler technique — Step-by-step for your device
• Trigger detective — Paste your symptom diary
• Exercise with asthma — Warm-up, timing, sport picks
• Season & travel prep — Pollen, colds, cold snaps, flights

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
