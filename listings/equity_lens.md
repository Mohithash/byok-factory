# Equity Lens

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.equitylens`  
**Short description (≤80):** Option pools, vesting and dilution made plain

## Full description
Equity Lens explains startup equity in plain English: it sizes option pools, drafts grant summaries, models dilution across rounds, proposes founder splits and helps you explain an offer to a candidate or employee.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Size the option pool — How big, for how many hires
• Draft a grant — Numbers + a plain-English summary
• Model dilution — Founders' stake through the rounds
• Explain equity to… — Candidate, employee, team or co-founder
• Founder split — A fair split with vesting

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
