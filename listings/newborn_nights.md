# Newborn Nights

**Category:** Parenting  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.newbornnights`  
**Short description (≤80):** Survive newborn nights with a plan, not guesswork

## Full description
Newborn Nights gets families through the first four months of newborn sleep: wake-window schedules for today, a safe-sleep check of your cot from a photo, fair night-shift splits between carers and fixes for day–night confusion.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Today's schedule — Wake windows + naps from wake-up time
• Safe-sleep check — Photo of the cot or bassinet
• Night-shift split — A fair plan for two tired adults
• Day–night confusion — Baby parties at 2am
• The night put-down — Fed, calm-ish, but wakes the second they touch the cot

FEATURES

• Follow-up questions in a thread that remembers the conversation
• Regenerate any answer, or edit your input and run it again
• Answers in 40+ languages, brief, standard or detailed
• Standing instructions applied to every answer (e.g. "metric units")

• Favourites, notes, search and filters for everything you save

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
