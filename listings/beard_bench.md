# Beard Bench

**Category:** Beauty  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.beardbench`  
**Short description (≤80):** Grow, shape and keep a beard that suits your face

## Full description
Beard Bench picks the beard shape for your face, plans growth through the patchy weeks, guides trimming line by line with guard numbers, builds a product routine and gives you the exact words to say to a barber.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Which beard suits me? — Photo → shapes for your face
• Growth plan — Survive the patchy weeks
• Trim it yourself — Lines, guards, order
• Product routine — Wash, oil, balm, brush
• Tell the barber — The exact words

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
