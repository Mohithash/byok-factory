# Badge Bound

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.badgebound`  
**Short description (≤80):** Police, fire and EMS hiring from written test to oral board

## Full description
Badge Bound guides police, firefighter, paramedic, EMT, corrections and dispatch candidates through the hiring pipeline: process maps, written exam prep, physical ability test plans, background packet and polygraph preparation, and oral board practice.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Map the process — Every stage, timeline and what fails people
• Written exam prep — Reading, report writing, judgement, maths
• Physical test plan — CPAT, POPAT, PAT and academy fitness
• Background & polygraph — Disclose well, omit nothing
• Oral board practice — Why us, ethics, use of force, teamwork

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
