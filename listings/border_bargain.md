# Border Bargain

**Category:** Shopping  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.borderbargain`  
**Short description (≤80):** Shop abroad and at the airport without overpaying

## Full description
Border Bargain tells you whether duty-free is actually cheaper, walks you through tax-free refund paperwork, checks customs allowances before you land, and warns you before buying electronics abroad that won't work or aren't covered at home.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Duty-free worth it? — Airport price vs home price
• Tax-free refund walkthrough — Get the VAT / GST back
• Customs allowance check — Will this get through?
• Buying tech abroad — Warranty, plugs, bands and region locks
• True cost in my money — Currency, card fees and DCC

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
