# Clean Rota

**Category:** House & Home  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.cleanrota`  
**Short description (≤80):** A cleaning schedule your household will actually keep

## Full description
Clean Rota builds weekly and monthly cleaning schedules sized to your home and energy, splits chores fairly between housemates, and plans deep cleans and 30-minute panic tidies.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Build my rota — Weekly + monthly schedule
• Split the chores — Fair shares for housemates or family
• Deep-clean a room — Top-to-bottom method for one room
• Guests in 30 minutes — The panic tidy, prioritised
• What product for this? — The right cleaner for the surface or mess

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
