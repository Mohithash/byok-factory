# Cradle Costs

**Category:** Finance  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.cradlecosts`  
**Short description (≤80):** Budget for the baby before the baby arrives

## Full description
Cradle Costs prices the first year of a baby, plans the income dip during parental leave month by month, works out whether going back to work pays after childcare, trims the buy-list to what newborns actually need, and lists the support to check for.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• The buy list, trimmed — Need, nice, skip
• Parental-leave cash plan — The income dip, month by month
• Back to work or not? — Childcare vs salary, honestly
• First-year monthly budget — What a baby costs each month
• What we're entitled to — Benefits, leave and grants to check
