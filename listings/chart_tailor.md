# Chart Tailor

**Category:** Tools  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.charttailor`  
**Short description (≤80):** The right chart, styled so the point lands

## Full description
Chart Tailor picks the chart type that answers your question, redesigns your existing chart from a screenshot, builds colour-blind-safe palettes from your brand and writes the titles and annotations that make the point obvious.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Which chart? — Describe the data and the point
• Redesign my chart — Screenshot → concrete fixes
• Colour palette — Accessible colours that match your brand
• Title & annotations — Words that make the chart self-explanatory
• Chart set for a report — Which charts, in what order

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
