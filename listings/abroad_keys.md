# Overseas Keys

**Category:** Finance  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.abroadkeys`  
**Short description (≤80):** Buy property abroad with your eyes open

## Full description
Overseas Keys walks you through buying a home in another country: the local process and players, the true cost of purchase, currency and financing choices, questions for the lawyer, and what it costs to own from afar.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• How buying works there — Stages, players, when you're committed
• True cost of buying — Taxes, fees, currency, total cash
• Currency & financing — Spot, forward, staged, local loan
• Question the lawyer — Title, permits, debts, boundaries
• Owning from afar — Running costs, letting, keyholding

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
