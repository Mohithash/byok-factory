# Count Me In

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.countmein`  
**Short description (≤80):** Early maths in the kitchen, car and bath

## Full description
Count Me In builds number sense in 3–7-year-olds through play: subitising, counting, number bonds, shapes and measuring, with games from everyday moments, quick check-ins to see what your child truly understands, and fixes for the classic wobbles.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Maths right now — A game from wherever you are
• Teach a skill — One concept, hands-on, one week
• Understanding check-in — Find what they really know in 5 minutes
• Worksheet to play — Photo → hands-on version
• Number story — A maths story starring your child
