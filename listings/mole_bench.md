## Full description
Mole Bench balances equations, runs stoichiometry and limiting-reagent problems, plans solution dilutions, names compounds and predicts reaction products, with the working shown at every line.

• Predict the reaction — Products, type, what you'd see
• Practice set — 10 questions on one skill
