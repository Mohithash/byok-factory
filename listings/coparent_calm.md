# Co-Parent Calm

**Category:** Parenting  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.coparentcalm`  
**Short description (≤80):** Raise the kids together, even apart

## Full description
Co-Parent Calm helps separated parents keep messages businesslike, build custody and holiday schedules that work, handle disagreements, and explain changes to the kids in words that don't wound.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Rewrite my message — Brief, informative, friendly, firm
• Build a schedule — 2-2-3, week-on/off, or custom
• Holiday plan — Birthdays, school breaks, festivals
• We disagree — School, screens, medical, new partners
• Tell the kids — Explaining changes at their level

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
