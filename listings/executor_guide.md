# Executor Guide

**Category:** Tools  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.executorguide`  
**Short description (≤80):** Named executor? Here's what to do, in order

## Full description
Executor Guide walks a newly appointed executor or next of kin through the weeks after a death: the first tasks, decoding the will, notifying banks and agencies, building the estate inventory, and keeping beneficiaries informed.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• First 30 days — What matters now, what can wait
• Decode the will — Photo or paste → who gets what
• Notification letters — Banks, pensions, utilities, subscriptions
• Estate inventory — Assets, debts, values
• Update the beneficiaries — Keep everyone calm and informed

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
