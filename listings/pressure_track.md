# Pressure Track

**Category:** Medical  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.pressuretrack`  
**Short description (≤80):** Understand your blood pressure, one reading at a time

## Full description
Pressure Track turns home blood-pressure readings into clear averages and trends, checks your measuring technique, decodes sodium on labels and builds DASH-style meal plans.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Analyse my readings — Paste a week or a month
• Am I measuring right? — Fix the common mistakes
• Sodium label check — Photo → salt verdict
• DASH-style meal plan — 3 days, under 1,500 mg sodium
• Why was it high today? — Work through the likely causes

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
