# Nature Detective

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.naturedetective`  
**Short description (≤80):** Backyard science: hunts, IDs and a nature journal

## Full description
Nature Detective turns walks and gardens into science: scavenger hunts by season and place, photo identification of bugs, leaves, tracks and rocks pitched for kids, nature-journal prompts, and long-running investigations like a bird count or a bean race.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Scavenger hunt — A hunt for today's walk
• What did we find? — Photo → kid-level ID
• Nature journal page — Prompts to draw and write
• Long investigation — A multi-week science project
• This month outside — What's happening in nature now

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
