# Client Lines

**Category:** Communication  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.clientlines`  
**Short description (≤80):** The right message for every client moment

## Full description
Client Lines writes the messages freelancers dread: the weekly update, the 'we're going to be late' note, the reply to vague or conflicting feedback, the nudge when a client goes silent, and the welcome pack that sets expectations from day one.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Welcome pack — How I work, for new clients
• Weekly client update — Progress, next, needs
• We're going to be late — Deliver the delay with a plan
• Decode their feedback — Vague or conflicting notes → questions
• Client went quiet — Nudges that get a reply

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
