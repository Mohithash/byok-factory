# Still Minutes

**Category:** Health & Fitness  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.stillminutes`  
**Short description (≤80):** Meditation scripts written for you, not a crowd

## Full description
Still Minutes writes guided meditations for your exact time, goal and setting, fixes the classic problems (falling asleep, racing mind, boredom) and builds a four-week practice that grows with you.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Write a meditation — Read it or record it
• Fix my practice — Sleepy, restless, bored, sore
• Everyday mindfulness — Practice without sitting
• 4-week progression — Build the habit gently

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
