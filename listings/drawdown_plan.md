# Drawdown Plan

**Category:** Finance  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.drawdownplan`  
**Short description (≤80):** Turn a nest egg into a paycheck that lasts

## Full description
Drawdown Plan is for the last five years before retirement and the first ten after: withdrawal rates that fit your horizon, bucket strategies, sequence-risk protection and a year-one income schedule you can actually follow.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Pick my withdrawal rate — How much can the pot pay?
• Build my buckets — Cash, income, growth
• Year-one paycheck — A monthly income schedule
• Protect against a crash — Sequence risk, defended
• Annual check-up — Raise, hold or cut?

• Regenerate any answer, or edit your input and run it again

• Standing instructions applied to every answer (e.g. "metric units")

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
