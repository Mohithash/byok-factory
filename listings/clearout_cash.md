# Clearout Cash

**Category:** Shopping  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.clearoutcash`  
**Short description (≤80):** Turn the stuff you don't use into money

## Full description
Clearout Cash triages a room of unused stuff into sell, donate or bin, prices each item from a photo, writes listings for the right platform, answers lowballers and time-wasters for you, and gets it packed and posted without getting scammed.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Triage a clearout — List everything → sell, donate or bin
• Price it — Photo → what it'll actually fetch
• Write the listing — Title, description and keywords
• Handle buyers — Paste a message → your reply
• Pack & post — Get it there in one piece

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
