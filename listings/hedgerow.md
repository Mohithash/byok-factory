# Hedgerow

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.hedgerow`  
**Short description (≤80):** Forage by the season, identify with care, cook the haul

## Full description
Hedgerow is a foraging companion: what's out this month near you, photo identification with lookalikes and a confidence check, what to do with a basket of the good stuff, and the etiquette and law of picking politely.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• What's out now — Seasonal calendar for your region
• Is this what I think it is? — Photo → ID, lookalikes, confidence
• Cook the haul — Recipes and preserves
• Pick politely — Rules, etiquette, sustainability
• Plan a foraging walk — Route, kit, targets

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
