# Nib & Ink

**Category:** Art & Design  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.nibandink`  
**Short description (≤80):** Calligraphy strokes, critiques and commissions

## Full description
Nib & Ink teaches pointed-pen, brush-lettering and broad-edge scripts stroke by stroke, critiques your practice sheets from a photo, plans wedding envelopes and signage, and sorts out inks, nibs and paper that misbehave.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Today's drill — Strokes, reps and guideline settings
• Critique my sheet — Photo → letter-by-letter feedback
• Plan a project — Envelopes, place cards, signs, quotes
• Fix tool trouble — Blobbing, scratching, feathering, fraying
• Lay out a piece — Composition, hierarchy, flourishes
