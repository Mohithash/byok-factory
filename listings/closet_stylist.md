# Closet Stylist

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.closetstylist`  
**Short description (≤80):** Outfits from the clothes you already own

## Full description
Closet Stylist builds outfits from your wardrobe photos, plans capsule wardrobes, and tells you what to pack or buy next.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Dress me — Occasion + weather → outfits
• Audit my closet — Photo → keep/alter/donate
• Capsule wardrobe — 30 pieces, 100 outfits
• Pack for a trip — Nothing unworn

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.

## Tools
- 👗 **Dress me** — Occasion + weather → outfits
- 📷 **Audit my closet** — Photo → keep/alter/donate
- 🧳 **Capsule wardrobe** — 30 pieces, 100 outfits
- ✈️ **Pack for a trip** — Nothing unworn
