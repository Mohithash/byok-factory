## Full description
Vert Runner coaches trail and mountain running: course recon from an elevation profile, vert-focused training weeks, descending and power-hiking skills, and mandatory-kit checks before race day.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Course recon — Profile → pacing and effort plan
• Vert-focused week — Build climbing legs
• Descending & hiking skills — Drills for technical terrain
• Mandatory-kit check — Photo → race-ready or not
• Race-week plan — Taper, travel, start line
