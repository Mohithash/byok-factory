# Feast & Famine

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.feastfamine`  
**Short description (≤80):** Know which projects fit before you say yes

## Full description
Feast & Famine helps freelancers decide whether they can take a project, stack multiple client deadlines into a workable week, fill a quiet month with pipeline work, hand part of the load to a subcontractor without losing margin, and renegotiate calmly when overbooked.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Can I take it? — Capacity check for a new project
• Stack my deadlines — One week, several clients
• Slow month playbook — Fill the pipeline when it's quiet
• Hand some of it off — Subcontract without losing margin
• I'm overbooked — Renegotiate before you burn out

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
