# Review Writer

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.reviewwriter`  
**Short description (≤80):** Fair, evidence-based reviews without the dread

## Full description
Review Writer turns a year of scattered notes into fair, evidence-based performance reviews, checks your language for bias, calibrates ratings across the team and preps you to deliver the hard ones.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Draft a review — Notes + self-review → full draft
• Bias & vagueness check — Audit a draft before it's sent
• Calibrate my team — Consistent ratings across people
• Deliver a tough review — Script the conversation
• Next-cycle goals — Measurable goals for one person

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
