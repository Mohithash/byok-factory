# Watt Works

**Category:** Sports  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.wattworks`  
**Short description (≤80):** Train by power: FTP, intervals and race pacing

## Full description
Watt Works turns your power meter or smart trainer into a coach: it reads your FTP test, writes interval workouts and training blocks, and paces your next event by the numbers.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Read my FTP test — Ramp, 20-min or 2×8 → zones
• Build a workout — Intervals for a goal, in minutes and watts
• Plan a block — 4–6 weeks toward an event
• Pace my event — Watts to hold for a TT, climb or fondo
• Debrief a ride — Paste your ride numbers

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
