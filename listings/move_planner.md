# Move Master

**Category:** House & Home  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.moveplanner`  
**Short description (≤80):** Relocating without the chaos

## Full description
Move Master builds moving timelines, room-by-room packing plans, address-change checklists and first-week setup for a new city.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Moving timeline — From now to move day
• Packing plan — Room by room
• Address change list — Who to tell
• First week in a new place — Set up life fast

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
