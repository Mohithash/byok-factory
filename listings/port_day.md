# Port Day

**Category:** Travel & Local  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.portday`  
**Short description (≤80):** Cabins, port days and onboard budgets, sorted

## Full description
Port Day helps cruisers choose the right cabin, plan every port stop around the all-aboard time, decide between ship excursions and doing it yourself, pack for formal nights and keep the onboard bill under control.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Pick my cabin — Deck, position and category
• Plan a port day — Back on board with time to spare
• Ship tour or DIY? — Compare the excursion with going alone
• Pack for the cruise — Formal nights, port days and the sea day
• Onboard budget — Drinks packages, gratuities, extras

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
