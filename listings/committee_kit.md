# Committee Kit

**Category:** Social  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.committeekit`  
**Short description (≤80):** Run your club like it's not your second job

## Full description
Committee Kit is for the volunteers who keep clubs and societies going: it drafts AGM notices and agendas, turns scribbled notes into proper minutes, writes officer role descriptions, checks what your constitution is missing and produces a treasurer's report members can actually follow.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• AGM pack — Notice, agenda and the chair's script
• Notes → minutes — Rough notes into proper minutes
• Role descriptions — Recruit an officer and hand over cleanly
• Treasurer's report — Figures into a report members follow
• Constitution check — Find the gaps in your rules

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
