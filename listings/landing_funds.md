# Landing Funds

**Category:** Finance  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.landingfunds`  
**Short description (≤80):** Make your arrival savings last until the first paycheck

## Full description
Landing Funds helps newcomers who arrive with limited savings stretch them until the first wage: a landing runway in weeks, setup costs in must/should/later order, a cheap-start guide, a fair-price check for rent, deposits and fees, and what to do if the money runs short.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• My landing runway — Weeks until the money's gone
• Setup costs, in order — Must, should, later
• Cheap start guide — Furnish and feed a home for less
• Is this a fair price? — Rent, deposits, fees, plans
• Money is running out — Where help exists, what to do first

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
