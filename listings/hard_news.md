# Hard News

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.hardnews`  
**Short description (≤80):** Deliver layoffs and reorgs like a human

## Full description
Hard News gets managers through the announcements nobody wants to make — layoffs, reorgs, cancelled projects, pay freezes, leaders leaving — with honest scripts, question prep and a plan for the people who stay.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Announce a change — Decision, why, what changes, what's next
• The layoff conversation — Fifteen minutes with dignity
• The team that stays — The first two weeks after
• Prep the tough questions — Including the hostile ones
• Reorg transition plan — Everything that breaks when lines move

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
