# Calc Climb

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.calcclimb`  
**Short description (≤80):** Limits to series, worked step by step with the rule named

## Full description
Calc Climb works derivatives, integrals, limits and series with every rule named, sets up related-rates and optimisation word problems, sketches curves from f, f′ and f″, and builds timed drills before exams.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Work a problem — Photo or typed
• Set up a word problem — Related rates & optimisation
• Series & convergence — Which test, and why
• Curve sketch — f, f′, f″ → the shape
• Timed drill — Exam-style set with answers

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
