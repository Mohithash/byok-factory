# Band Nine

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.bandnine`  
**Short description (≤80):** IELTS writing and speaking marked like an examiner would

## Full description
Band Nine scores your IELTS Writing Task 1 and Task 2 against the public band descriptors, runs Speaking Part 2 cue cards with model answers, drills each reading question type and builds topic vocabulary with collocations.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Mark my Task 2 essay — Band per criterion + the edits that lift it
• Task 1 from a chart — Photo of the prompt → overview, plan, phrases
• Speaking Part 2 cue card — Plan, model answer, Part 3 follow-ups
• Reading question types — Method, traps and a mini practice set
• Topic vocabulary — Words, collocations and a sample answer

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
