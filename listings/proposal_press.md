# Proposal Press

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.proposalpress`  
**Short description (≤80):** Proposals that read like the client wrote them

## Full description
Proposal Press turns discovery notes into client-centred proposals, executive summaries, pricing options and scopes of work, then reviews your draft like a sceptical procurement lead.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Draft a proposal — Notes → full structure
• Executive summary — Page one, rewritten
• Pricing options — Good / better / best
• Scope of work — Tight scope, no scope creep
• Procurement review — Paste a draft, get torn apart kindly

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
