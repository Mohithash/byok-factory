# Gene & Cell

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.genecell`  
**Short description (≤80):** Biology from Punnett squares to pathways, exam-ready

## Full description
Gene & Cell solves genetics crosses, walks cell and metabolic pathways stage by stage, identifies microscope slides from a photo, builds compare-and-contrast tables and plans long-answer exam questions.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Genetics cross — Punnett, dihybrid, pedigree, chi-square
• Pathway walkthrough — Mitosis, Krebs, translation…
• Identify a slide — Photo of a micrograph or specimen
• Compare & contrast — Two things, one clean table
• Plan a long answer — 6–10 mark questions

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
