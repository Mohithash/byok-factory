# Stroller Miles

**Category:** Travel & Local  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.strollermiles`  
**Short description (≤80):** Travel with babies and toddlers, nap by nap

## Full description
Stroller Miles plans trips around little ones: itineraries with nap windows, hour-by-hour flight survival plans, what to check before booking a stay, gear and snack packing lists and a playbook for meltdowns far from home.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Kid-paced itinerary — Days that fit around sleep
• Flight survival — Hour-by-hour with kids
• Check a stay — Is this place kid-ready?
• Gear & snack list — Everything, nothing extra
• Meltdown playbook — Right now, in public, far from home

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
