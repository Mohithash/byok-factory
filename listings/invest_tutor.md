# Invest Tutor

**Category:** Finance  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.investtutor`  
**Short description (≤80):** Investing explained from zero, one lesson at a time

## Full description
Invest Tutor teaches investing from scratch: plain-English lessons pitched at your level, any term explained with a worked example, headlines translated for long-term investors, and quizzes that check you actually got it.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Today's lesson — Five minutes on one idea
• Explain a term — Jargon → plain English + example
• Decode a headline — What the news means for a long-term investor
• Quiz me — Check you really understood
• Myth or fact? — Test something you heard

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
