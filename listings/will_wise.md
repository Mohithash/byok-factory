# Will Wise

**Category:** Tools  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.willwise`  
**Short description (≤80):** Understand wills before you write one

## Full description
Will Wise explains what a will does, what happens without one, how to choose executors and guardians, what to include, and when to update it — so you arrive at a solicitor or will-writing service already knowing what you want.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• If I died without a will — Who would inherit, roughly
• Choose executors and guardians — The people, not the paperwork
• My will worksheet — Everything a drafter will ask
• Does my will need updating? — Life changed; did the paper?
• Fair shares — Balance gifts across kids, step-kids and a partner

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
