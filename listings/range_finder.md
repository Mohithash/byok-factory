# Range Finder

**Category:** Health & Fitness  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.rangefinder`  
**Short description (≤80):** Mobility that fixes your squat, press and hinge

## Full description
Range Finder is mobility for people who lift: it screens your squat and overhead positions from a photo, finds the joint limiting a lift, and writes pre-lift warm-ups and 4-week range programmes for ankles, hips, thoracic spine and shoulders.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Screen a position — Photo at the bottom of a squat or overhead
• Fix a restriction — Depth, lockout, heels, butt wink…
• Pre-lift warm-up — Minutes tailored to today's lift
• 4-week range programme — Daily minutes, one joint at a time
• Log a retest — Before and after numbers into a verdict

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
