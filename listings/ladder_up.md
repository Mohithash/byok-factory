# Ladder Up

**Category:** House & Home  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.ladderup`  
**Short description (≤80):** From first deposit to first keys, step by step

## Full description
Ladder Up guides first-time buyers from working out what they can really afford, through decoding listings and making offers, to the weeks between an accepted offer and the keys.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• What can I afford? — Price ceiling, monthly cost, buffer
• Deposit plan — Months to target and how to shorten them
• Decode a listing — Screenshot → agent-speak translated
• Make an offer — Three offer levels and the exact words
• Offer to keys — Every stage, who acts, what can slip

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
