# Vamos Spanish

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.vamosspanish`  
**Short description (≤80):** Travel Spanish for the country you're actually visiting

## Full description
Vamos Spanish teaches the Spanish they actually speak where you're going — Mexico, Spain, Argentina or Colombia — with situation scripts, menu decoding and the replies you'll hear back.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Situation script — The dialogue with likely replies
• Decode a menu or sign — Photo → what it says and what to pick
• What did they say? — Type what you heard, get the reply
• Say it like a local — One phrase, four countries
• Tomorrow's 10 words — Vocabulary for the plan

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
