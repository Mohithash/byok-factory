# Send Home

**Category:** Finance  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.sendhome`  
**Short description (≤80):** Support family back home without sinking your own budget

## Full description
Send Home helps immigrants balance remittances to family with rent, savings and a future here: a send-home amount you can sustain, kind but firm scripts for relatives' requests, building credit from zero, decoding your payslip and spotting the scams aimed at newcomers.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• My send-home plan — How much to send, sustainably
• A request from family — Say yes, no or not yet, kindly
• Build credit from zero — No history here yet
• Decode my payslip — Photo → what every line means
• Is this a scam? — Paste the message, call or offer

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
