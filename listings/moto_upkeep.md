# Throttle Log

**Category:** Auto & Vehicles  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.motoupkeep`  
**Short description (≤80):** Keep your motorcycle ready for every ride

## Full description
Throttle Log runs pre-ride checks, builds a service schedule for your exact bike and mileage, diagnoses noises and symptoms, reads chain and tyre wear from a photo and walks you through winter lay-up.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Pre-ride check — T-CLOCS in 5 minutes
• Service schedule — What's due at your mileage
• Diagnose a symptom — Noise, vibration, stall, leak
• Chain & tyre check — Photo → wear verdict
• Store & wake up — Winter lay-up and spring return

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
