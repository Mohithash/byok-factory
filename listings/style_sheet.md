# Style Sheet

**Category:** Tools  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.stylesheet`  
**Short description (≤80):** Consistency for long manuscripts, chapter by chapter

## Full description
Style Sheet builds a copyeditor's style sheet from your manuscript, checks new chapters against it, converts between US and UK conventions, scans for continuity slips and trims to a word count.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Build my style sheet — Decisions extracted from a sample
• Check a chapter — Find every departure from the sheet
• US ↔ UK convert — Spelling, punctuation and vocabulary
• Continuity scan — Names, ages, eye colours, timelines
• Trim to length — Cut to a word count without losing the voice

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
