# Chess Coach

**Category:** Games  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.chesscoach`  
**Short description (≤80):** Openings, tactics and post-game reviews

## Full description
Chess Coach builds an opening repertoire for your level, drills tactics, reviews your games from PGN and explains plans in plain language.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Build a repertoire — For your level
• Review my game — Paste PGN or moves
• Tactics drill — 10 patterns
• What's the plan? — Describe a position

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.

## Tools
- 📚 **Build a repertoire** — For your level
- 🔍 **Review my game** — Paste PGN or moves
- ⚔️ **Tactics drill** — 10 patterns
- 🧭 **What's the plan?** — Describe a position
