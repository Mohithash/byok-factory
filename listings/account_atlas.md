# Account Atlas

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.accountatlas`  
**Short description (≤80):** Strategic account plans that actually get used

## Full description
Account Atlas builds one-page account plans, stakeholder maps, whitespace analyses and QBR agendas for the accounts that matter most, and tells you which ones deserve your hours.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Account plan — One page, one quarter
• Stakeholder map — Who matters, who's missing
• Whitespace analysis — What else could they buy
• QBR agenda — A business review they'll show up to
• Prioritise my book — Which accounts deserve the hours

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
