# First Bites

**Category:** Parenting  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.firstbites`  
**Short description (≤80):** Solids made simple, from first spoon to family plate

## Full description
First Bites plans the first weeks of solids, schedules allergen introductions, turns tonight's dinner into a baby-safe version from a photo, and progresses textures as your baby grows.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 2-week starter plan — First foods, day by day
• Allergen ladder — Introduce the top allergens safely
• Baby-size our dinner — Photo of your plate → baby version
• Texture progression — From smooth to lumps to finger foods
• Won't eat solids — Turns head, throws food, wants milk

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
