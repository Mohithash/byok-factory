# Stat Check

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.statcheck`  
**Short description (≤80):** Pick the right test, read the output, write it up

## Full description
Stat Check picks the statistical test for your design, decodes SPSS, R or Python output into plain English, sizes your sample, critiques charts from a photo and writes APA-style results paragraphs.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Which test? — Describe the design, get the test
• Interpret my output — Paste output or a screenshot
• Sample size & power — How many participants?
• Describe my data — Paste numbers → summary and the right plot
• Critique a chart — Photo of a graph → is it honest?

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
