# Passage Gym

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.passagegym`  
**Short description (≤80):** Reading comprehension drills for SAT, ACT, GRE and IELTS

## Full description
Passage Gym turns any passage into exam-style questions for your test, teaches the reading strategy each question type needs, drills vocabulary in context and checks your reading speed against the test's real pace.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Passage → questions — Paste anything, get exam-style questions
• Fresh practice passage — Original passage in your test's style
• Question-type strategy — Method, kill rules, worked example
• Reading speed check — WPM vs what your test demands
• Explain a missed question — Where the answer lived and why you missed it

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
