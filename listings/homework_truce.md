# Homework Truce

**Category:** Parenting  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.homeworktruce`  
**Short description (≤80):** End the nightly homework fight without doing it for them

## Full description
Homework Truce tackles the fight, not the fractions: it chunks assignments into timed sprints, sets up the workspace, builds motivation systems that don't backfire, and plans long projects backwards from the due date.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Break the standoff — They won't start. Right now.
• Chunk this assignment — Photo or paste → timed sprints
• Homework station — Photo of where they work
• Motivation system — Rewards that don't backfire
• Long project planner — Backwards from the due date

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
