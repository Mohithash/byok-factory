# Flow Builder

**Category:** Health & Fitness  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.yogaflow`  
**Short description (≤80):** Yoga sequences for how you feel right now

## Full description
Flow Builder writes yoga and mobility sequences for your time, level and mood, with cues, modifications and a breathing practice to match.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Build a flow — Tell me time + mood
• Fix a pose — Photo or description
• Breathwork — 5 minutes to shift state
• 7-day challenge — A week of short practices

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
