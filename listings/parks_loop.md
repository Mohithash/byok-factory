# Parks Loop

**Category:** Travel & Local  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.parksloop`  
**Short description (≤80):** US national park road trips, park by park

## Full description
Parks Loop plans US national park road trips: a day-by-day loop with realistic drive hours, the timed-entry and permit reservations you must win months out, campground vs lodge choices, hikes matched to your group, and trailhead plans that respect heat, altitude and wildlife.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Build the loop — Days, drives and where to sleep
• Permits & timed entry — Win the reservations months out
• Camp or lodge? — Where to sleep each night
• Hikes for us — Matched to legs, lungs and time
• Trailhead day plan — Timed for parking, heat and daylight

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
