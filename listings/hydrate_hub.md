# Hydrate Hub

**Category:** Health & Fitness  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.hydratehub`  
**Short description (≤80):** Drink right for your body, sport and weather

## Full description
Hydrate Hub calculates daily fluid and electrolyte needs, builds race-day hydration plans, and decodes sports-drink labels.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• My daily target — Fluids + when
• Race-day plan — Marathon, cycle, hike, match
• DIY electrolyte drink — Cheap and tuned
• Decode a drink label — Photo → is it worth it

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.

## Tools
- 💧 **My daily target** — Fluids + when
- 🏁 **Race-day plan** — Marathon, cycle, hike, match
