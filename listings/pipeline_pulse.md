# Pipeline Pulse

**Category:** Business  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.pipelinepulse`  
**Short description (≤80):** Honest deal reviews and forecasts you can defend

## Full description
Pipeline Pulse inspects your deals for real qualification gaps, sorts your forecast into commit, best case and pipeline, revives stalled opportunities and preps you for the weekly forecast call.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Review my pipeline — Paste deals, get a ranked view
• Inspect a deal — Full qualification audit
• Revive a stalled deal — Silent for weeks
• Forecast call prep — Defend your number
• Weekly selling plan — Where the hours go

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
