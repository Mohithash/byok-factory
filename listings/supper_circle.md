# Supper Circle

**Category:** Social  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.suppercircle`  
**Short description (≤80):** A rotating dinner club that actually keeps going

## Full description
Supper Circle runs a rotating dinner club among friends or neighbours: a fair hosting rota, themed host briefs that respect every diet at the table, sensible money rules, a newcomer's first night and the nudges that keep the club alive when life gets busy.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Hosting rota — Who hosts when, fairly
• Host brief — Theme, menu and timings for the host
• Money rules — Keep it generous, not awkward
• Attendance rescue — When the chat goes quiet
• Add a newcomer — Their first dinner, done well

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
