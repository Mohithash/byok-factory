# Ready Kit

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.emergencyready`  
**Short description (≤80):** Prepare your household for emergencies

## Full description
Ready Kit builds go-bags and home kits for your risks, writes family emergency plans and checklists for storms, outages and evacuations.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Build my kit — Home or go-bag
• Family plan — Who does what
• Something's coming — Storm, outage, heatwave
• Afterwards — Recovery steps

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.

## Tools
- 🎒 **Build my kit** — Home or go-bag
- 📋 **Family plan** — Who does what
- ⚠️ **Something's coming** — Storm, outage, heatwave
- 🔄 **Afterwards** — Recovery steps
