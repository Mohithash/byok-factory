# Mortgage Lens

**Category:** Finance  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.mortgagelens`  
**Short description (≤80):** See the real cost of any mortgage before you sign

## Full description
Mortgage Lens compares mortgage offers on total cost, runs overpayment and stress-test scenarios, times your remortgage and translates every term on the paperwork into plain English.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Compare offers — Paste 2–3 offers, see total cost
• Overpayment maths — Interest saved, years cut
• Fixed or variable? — Rate paths for your risk tolerance
• Remortgage timing — From deal end to switch
• Decode the paperwork — ERC, APRC, LTV… in plain English

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
