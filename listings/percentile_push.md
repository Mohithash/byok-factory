# Percentile Push

**Category:** Education  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.percentilepush`  
**Short description (≤80):** GRE vocab, quant comparisons and essays, drilled to a score

## Full description
Percentile Push drills GRE Verbal (text completion, sentence equivalence, reading) and Quant (quantitative comparison, data interpretation) in exam format, builds root-based vocabulary sets and grades your Analyze an Issue essays on the 0–6 scale.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Verbal drill — Text completion, sentence equivalence, reading
• Quant comparison drill — Quantity A vs B, plus data and algebra
• Root-based vocab set — 12 words from one root or theme
• Grade my Issue essay — 0–6 score with the fixes that raise it
• Study plan — Weeks, hours and milestones to your targets

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
