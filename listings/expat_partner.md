# Expat Partner

**Category:** Travel & Local  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.expatpartner`  
**Short description (≤80):** Rebuild career, community and self after the move

## Full description
Expat Partner is for the person who moved for someone else's job: rebuilding a career that can travel, finding your people, structuring weeks that have lost their shape, and keeping the relationship honest along the way.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Career options here — Local, remote, portable, retrain
• Find my people — A 4-week plan to build community
• Shape my week — Structure when no one needs you at 9am
• Talk to my partner — Say the hard thing kindly
• Who am I here? — Reframe the gap nobody planned

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
