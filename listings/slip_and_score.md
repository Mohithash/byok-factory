# Slip & Score

**Category:** Art & Design  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.slipandscore`  
**Short description (≤80):** Throw, glaze and fire with fewer cracks

## Full description
Slip & Score is a potter's studio companion: throwing and hand-building diagnoses from a photo, glaze combos and firing schedules for your kiln, projects sized to your skill, and clay-body, cone and studio-safety questions answered.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• What went wrong? — Photo → defect, cause, prevention
• Plan a project — Weights, dimensions, shrinkage, steps
• Glaze & firing — Combos, coats and a firing schedule
• Practice session — Centring, pulling, trimming, handles
• Studio & safety — Recycling clay, dust, kilns, materials
