# Ply & Volt

**Category:** Auto & Vehicles

## Full description
Ply & Volt plans your DIY campervan conversion: furniture build plans with cut lists, a wired-and-fused 12 V system sized to what you run, insulation and ventilation choices, a build order with budget, and the paperwork to make it legal.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Furniture build plan — One unit → dimensions, cut list, fixings
• Size the electrics — Solar, battery, inverter, wire
• Insulation & ventilation — Warm, dry, no condensation
• Build order & budget — What comes before what
• Make it legal — Registration, insurance, weights
