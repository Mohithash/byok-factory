# Wedding Wiz

**Category:** Lifestyle  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.weddingwiz`  
**Short description (≤80):** Plan the day without losing your mind

## Full description
Wedding Wiz builds timelines, budgets, vendor questions, seating plans and speeches, from proposal to last dance.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Planning timeline — From now to the day
• Allocate the budget — Where the money goes
• Vendor questions — Don't get burned
• Write a speech — Vows, best man, parent

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
