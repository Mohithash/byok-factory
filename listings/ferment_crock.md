# Crock & Brine

**Category:** Food & Drink  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.fermentcrock`  
**Short description (≤80):** Sauerkraut, pickles and hot sauce, by the numbers

## Full description
Crock & Brine calculates salt by weight, writes ferment recipes for kraut, pickles, hot sauce and garlic honey, tracks timelines for your kitchen temperature, and tells you from a photo whether that film is kahm or mould.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Brine calculator — Exact salt for your jar
• Ferment recipe — Kraut, pickles, carrots, salsa…
• Kahm or mould? — Photo of the jar surface
• Hot sauce builder — From mash to bottle
• Timeline & storage — When it's ready, how long it keeps

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
