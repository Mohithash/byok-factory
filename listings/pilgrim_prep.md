## Full description
Pilgrim Prep helps you prepare for Hajj or Umrah, the Camino de Santiago, Char Dham, Kumbh Mela, Shikoku's 88 temples, Lourdes, Bodh Gaya and more — the sequence of rites, packing, physical training and a realistic budget.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• The rites in order — What happens, when, and why
• Packing list — By climate, rite and body
• Body prep plan — Weeks of walking, heat and stairs
• Budget & bookings — Costs, timing, what to book first
• Crowd & safety plan — Stay together, stay calm, stay hydrated
