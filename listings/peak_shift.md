# Peak Shift

**Category:** House & Home  
**Package:** `com.mohithash.byok.peakshift`  
**Short description (≤80):** Run things when power is cheap, and cut the standby

## Full description
Peak Shift works out what each appliance really costs to run, builds a daily plan to shift washing, dishwashing, charging and heating into off-peak or solar hours, checks whether a time-of-use tariff would pay, and hunts down phantom standby loads.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• What does it cost to run? — Wattage or rating plate → cost per use
• Shift my day — When to run everything for the cheapest day
• Would a different tariff pay? — Time-of-use, EV or solar-export tariffs
• Standby hunt — Find the phantom loads worth killing
• Use my own solar — Self-consumption with or without a battery

BRING YOUR OWN KEY
Works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Ollama…). Your key and everything you write stay on your phone; requests go straight from your device to the provider you chose. No accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Every answer is a structured, shareable document with follow-up questions, saved on-device so you can come back to it.
